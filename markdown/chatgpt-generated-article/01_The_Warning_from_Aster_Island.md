# 1. The Warning from Aster Island（阿斯特岛的警报）

**主题：** 自然地理　 **目标词：** 41

Dr. Lin arrived on Aster Island, a volcanic island rising sharply from the Pacific. Her task was to study how the **hydrosphere** and the **lithosphere** continually reshaped its **landscape**. After recording the station's **longitude** and **latitude**, she looked through the morning **mist** toward the **horizon**. The coast consisted of dark **granite**, crossed by veins of white **quartz** and iron **ore**. When a small **pebble** clung to her **magnet**, she labelled it and carried it back to the research station.

The station's **greenhouse** also served as a laboratory. Cylinders of **hydrogen** supplied weather balloons, while a tray held samples of red **oxide**. On a screen, a **thermodynamic** model traced heat rising from Earth's **core**, through the **mantle** and **crust**, toward a chamber of **magma** beneath the island. That year, **El Nino** had weakened the summer **monsoon**, and a long **drought** had dried the forest. **Smog** covered the harbour, and a bitter **fume** drifted from distant fires. By noon, a light **breeze** became a violent **gust**, then a **gale**. Over the sea, a narrow **tornado** touched the water.

Suddenly, the instruments registered a strong **seismic** pulse. An underwater slope collapsed, triggering a **tsunami**. Sirens sounded as **flooding** cut the coastal road and a muddy **torrent** rushed across the lower **terrain**. High above the village, rain and vibration released an **avalanche** of snow and rock from the island's high summit. At the same moment, a **mishap** shattered a hydrogen valve inside the greenhouse. Lin wanted to return for her samples, but the station chief stopped her. The leaking gas could **endanger** her life and **jeopardise** the entire rescue. What began as harsh weather had become a **destructive**, **catastrophic** chain of events; another delay could turn it into an even greater **calamity**.

Lin abandoned the samples but took the drive containing her data. She guided the villagers to shelters on solid high ground and transmitted her measurements to the mainland. The tsunami destroyed the station, yet the evacuation succeeded. Weeks later, scientists used her records to improve the island's warning system. Lin understood that the disaster had not been a collection of unrelated accidents: air, water, rock, heat, and human decisions had formed one connected story.

## 中文翻译

林博士来到阿斯特岛。这是一座从太平洋中陡然升起的火山岛。她的任务是研究**水圈**和**岩石圈**如何持续改变岛屿的**地貌**。记录完研究站的**经度**和**纬度**后，她透过清晨的**薄雾**望向**地平线**。海岸由深色**花岗岩**构成，岩石间穿插着白色**石英**和铁**矿石**的矿脉。一颗小**鹅卵石**被她的**磁铁**吸住，她便贴好标签，把它带回研究站。

研究站的**温室**同时也是实验室。几只**氢气**瓶为气象气球供气，托盘里则放着红色**氧化物**样本。屏幕上的**热力学**模型显示，热量从地球**地核**向上穿过**地幔**和**地壳**，抵达岛屿下方的**岩浆**腔。那一年，**厄尔尼诺现象**削弱了夏季**季风**，长期**干旱**使森林变得异常干燥。港口上空笼罩着**雾霾**，远处火灾产生的刺鼻**烟气**随风飘来。中午时，一阵轻柔的**微风**变成猛烈的**阵风**，继而发展为**烈风**。海面上，一股狭长的**龙卷风**触及水面。

突然，仪器记录到一次强烈的**地震活动**。水下斜坡坍塌，引发了**海啸**。警报响起时，**洪水泛滥**切断了沿海道路，泥泞的**激流**冲过低处**地形**。在村庄上方的高山上，降雨和震动又引发了一场夹杂冰雪与岩石的**雪崩**。与此同时，温室里发生了一起**意外事故**，氢气阀门破裂。林博士想返回去抢救样本，却被站长拦住。泄漏的气体可能**危及**她的生命，并**危及**整个救援行动。原本的恶劣天气已经演变成一连串具有**破坏性**且**灾难性**的事件；再耽搁片刻，就可能酿成更大的**灾难**。

林博士放弃了样本，但带走了存有研究数据的硬盘。她把村民带到地势坚固的高处避难所，并将测量结果传送给大陆。海啸摧毁了研究站，但撤离行动成功了。几周后，科学家利用她的记录改进了岛上的预警系统。林博士终于明白，这场灾害并不是一连串互不相关的事故：空气、水、岩石、热量以及人的选择，共同构成了一个彼此关联的故事。
