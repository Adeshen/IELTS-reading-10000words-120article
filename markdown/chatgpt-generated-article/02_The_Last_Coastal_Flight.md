# 2. The Last Coastal Flight（最后一次海岸飞行）

**主题：** 自然地理　 **目标词：** 41

Mara was a young pilot assigned to carry medicine from the **mainland** to a fishing village on a remote **peninsula**. The old route followed the **coast**, past a white **cliff** and a **stony** **beach**, but an advancing **glacier** had changed the northern **terrain**. Her instructor warned that a **superficial** reading of the map could turn a simple flight into disaster. She therefore checked the station’s **meteorology** report and plotted the village’s position in the southern **hemisphere**, far from the **equator** but well outside the **polar** zone.

At first the flight was **smooth**, the weather was **mild**, and the **Atlantic** looked almost **pacific**. Then cabin **heating** began to fail, and salt air made the controls **deteriorate**. A **moderate** crosswind pushed the aircraft toward a **plateau** whose **vertical** wall rose above a **rough**, **sandy** **plain**. Turning west would **aggravate** the fuel problem; continuing north might **endanger** the engine. Mara chose to **upgrade** her route with live satellite data rather than trust the outdated chart. She adjusted the aircraft’s **axis** and crossed a narrow **gap** between two ridges.

Below, a **brook** joined a larger **stream**, which wound from its mountain **source** through a **swamp** and into a river **delta**. The water had begun to **erode** the banks and **degrade** the village’s only road. Using the river for **navigation**, Mara reached a calm **gulf** and landed beside an abandoned **marine** research station. The villagers arrived by boat and collected the medicine. That evening, an engineer explained that the glacier’s meltwater would be guided toward a dry **oasis** inland. Mara marked the new channels on charts shared with pilots across **Oceania**, the **Mediterranean**, and every neighbouring **ocean** route. Her last training flight became the map that kept future crews safe.

## 中文翻译

年轻飞行员玛拉接到任务，要把药品从**大陆**运往一个偏远**半岛**上的渔村。旧航线沿着**海岸**飞行，途中经过白色**悬崖**和一片遍布石块的**多石的****海滩**，但不断推进的**冰川**已经改变了北部**地形**。教官提醒她，只对地图作**肤浅的**解读，原本简单的飞行也可能酿成灾难。因此，她查看了气象站的**气象学**报告，并标出了村庄的位置：它处在南**半球**，远离**赤道**，但也并非**极地的**区域。

起初飞行十分**平稳**，天气很**温和**，**大西洋**也显得十分**平静**。随后机舱**供暖**开始失灵，盐分很高的空气又使操纵装置逐渐**恶化**。一股**中等的**侧风把飞机推向一座**高原**，它**垂直的**岩壁高悬在一片**崎岖的**、**沙质的****平原**上方。向西转会**加剧**燃油问题，继续向北则可能危及发动机。玛拉决定用实时卫星数据**升级**航线，而不再依赖过时的航图。她调整飞机的**轴线**，穿过两道山脊之间狭窄的**缺口**。

下方，一条**小溪**汇入更大的**溪流**，河水从山间**源头**出发，穿过**沼泽**，流入河流**三角洲**。水流开始**侵蚀**河岸，并使村里唯一的道路**退化**。玛拉以河流辅助**导航**，终于抵达一处平静的**海湾**，降落在废弃的**海洋的**研究站旁。村民乘船赶来取走药品。当晚，一名工程师告诉她，冰川融水将被引向内陆一处干燥的**绿洲**。玛拉把新水道标在航图上，并与**大洋洲**、**地中海**以及邻近各条**海洋**航线的飞行员共享。她的最后一次训练飞行，就这样变成了保护后来机组的地图。
