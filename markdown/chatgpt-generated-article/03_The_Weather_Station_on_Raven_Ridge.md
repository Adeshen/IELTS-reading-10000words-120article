# 3. The Weather Station on Raven Ridge（乌鸦岭气象站）

**主题：** 自然地理　 **目标词：** 41

Nadia managed a weather station on a **desolate** **hillside** outside a **rural** town. To the **northeast** stood a **snowy** **peak**; a long **ridge** ran **southeast**, while the road descended **southwest** toward a new **suburb** on the town’s **outskirts**. The surrounding soil was **arid**, yet an **adjacent** valley remained **moist** and **humid** because underground water rose through layers of **sediment**, **silt**, and dark **dirt**. Developers wanted to **mount** solar panels on the steep **slope**, but Nadia feared their plan ignored the mountain’s unstable weather.

One April afternoon, the temperature climbed to twenty degrees **Celsius**. **Warm** air from the **tropics** met a **frigid** current above the eastern valley. The station recorded a sudden **thermal** change: a gentle **sprinkle** became a **shower**, then a violent **downpour** mixed with **hail**. Nadia felt a sharp **chill** and began to **shiver** as the sky turned **stormy**. Snow began to **thaw** **irregularly**, sending water down channels that the builders had placed in an **inappropriate** direction.

The resulting landslide was not **inevitable**. Nadia radioed the crew, but a broken antenna made her message unclear. She ran downhill and reached them just as **toxic** liquid leaked from a fuel drum. Together they moved the drum and dug a trench, preventing the spill from entering the wet valley. The solar frames were damaged, but the environmental harm was not **irreversible**. Afterward, the company abandoned its rigid plan and asked Nadia to choose safer sites on the **northwest** side. Her report explained that the mountain’s **eastern**, sometimes called **oriental**, exposure received strong morning sun but also rapid temperature shifts. By respecting those patterns, the new station supplied clean power without poisoning the valley.

## 中文翻译

娜迪娅管理着一座气象站，它坐落在一座**荒凉的****山坡**上，位于某个**乡村的**小镇之外。**东北方**耸立着一座**积雪的****山峰**；一道长长的**山脊**伸向**东南方**，公路则向**西南方**下行，通往镇子**郊区**边缘的新**近郊住宅区**。周围土壤十分**干旱的**，但一个**毗邻的**山谷因为地下水穿过**沉积物**、**淤泥**和深色**泥土**不断上涌，依旧保持**湿润的**和**潮湿的**状态。开发商打算在陡峭**斜坡**上**安装**太阳能板，娜迪娅却担心他们忽略了山区多变的天气。

四月的一个下午，气温升至二十**摄氏度**。来自**热带地区**的**温暖的**空气与东部山谷上方的**严寒的**气流相遇。气象站记录到突然的**热量的**变化：轻微的**零星小雨**变成一阵**阵雨**，继而发展成夹着**冰雹**的猛烈**倾盆大雨**。天空变得**暴风雨的**，娜迪娅感到一阵刺骨的**寒意**，不禁开始**发抖**。积雪开始**不规则地****融化**，水流沿着施工人员设置在**不恰当的**方向上的排水沟奔流而下。

这场山体滑坡并非**不可避免的**。娜迪娅用无线电呼叫施工队，但损坏的天线使信息含混不清。她跑下山，赶到时一只油桶正泄漏**有毒的**液体。大家合力移走油桶、挖出沟渠，阻止污染物进入湿润山谷。太阳能支架虽然受损，环境损害却并非**不可逆转的**。事后，公司放弃僵化方案，请娜迪娅在**西北方**选择更安全的位置。她在报告中说明，山体**东部的**朝向——有时也称为**东方的**朝向——能获得强烈晨光，却也会经历快速温差。新气象站尊重这些规律，最终既提供了清洁电力，也没有毒害山谷。
