# 4. The Reservoir That Returned（重获生机的水库）

**主题：** 自然地理　 **目标词：** 41

When geologist Arun returned to his childhood valley, the **magnificent** **waterfall** had become a thin **drip**. A factory on the **fringe** of the **wilderness** had begun to **deforest** the upper valley. Its waste introduced a dangerous **pollutant** that could **contaminate** the main **reservoir**. A **dense** layer of **synthetic** foam floated beside broken machinery and other **debris**. The once **fertile** river **margin** was turning **barren**, and a widening **gap** had appeared in the rock **plate** above the dam.

Arun studied the valley’s **geology** and proposed an **intensive** restoration. Workers would **drain** polluted pools, remove waste, and **fertilize** damaged soil with natural compost. They installed **solar** pumps that made clean water **gush** from an underground **spring**. In sunlight, water would **evaporate** into **vapour**, then **precipitate** over the forest and refill the streams. Some officials called this merely a beautiful theory, but Arun used a **lunar** and seasonal **calendar** to show how rainfall **intensity** changed between **sunrise** and **sunset**.

During a rare **eclipse**, the crack above the dam suddenly released a **puff** of dust. Arun ordered an evacuation. Moments later, loose stones fell, yet the repaired barrier held. As the moon moved away, **sunshine** revealed only minor damage. Over the following year, seedlings began to **emerge**, wildlife returned, and the revived waterfall created a **dramatic** rainbow. Villagers joked that their valley looked like **paradise**, though Arun said no place was guaranteed by **heaven**; it survived because people respected **nature**. The project showed that technology and conservation were not opposites. Used carefully, pumps and data could restore the natural cycle instead of replacing it. By then, morning **dew** shone on the grass.

## 中文翻译

地质学家阿伦回到童年生活过的山谷时，那道曾经**壮丽的****瀑布**已经缩成细细的**滴水**。一家建在**荒野**的**边缘地带**的工厂开始**砍伐森林**。它排放的废物带来一种危险**污染物**，可能**污染**主要**水库**。一层**浓密的****合成的**泡沫漂浮在损坏的机器和其他**残骸**旁。昔日**肥沃的**河岸**边缘**正在变得**贫瘠的**，水坝上方的岩石**板块**还出现了一道不断扩大的**裂缝**。

阿伦研究山谷的**地质情况**，提出一套**密集的**修复方案。工人要**排干**受污染的水潭，清走废物，再用天然堆肥给受损土壤**施肥**。他们安装了**太阳能的**水泵，让清水从地下**泉水**中**涌出**。阳光下，水会**蒸发**成**水汽**，随后**降水**到森林上方，再次补充溪流。有些官员认为这只是漂亮的理论，但阿伦利用**月球的**周期和季节**日历**，说明降雨**强度**在**日出**与**日落**之间如何变化。

一次罕见的**日食**期间，水坝上方的裂缝突然喷出一**股**尘土。阿伦立刻下令疏散。片刻后，松动的石块坠落，但修复后的屏障经受住了冲击。月亮移开后，**阳光**照出了并不严重的损坏。随后一年里，幼苗开始**出现**，野生动物重新归来，复苏的瀑布形成一道**引人注目的**彩虹。村民开玩笑说山谷宛如**天堂**，阿伦却说，没有任何地方会由**上天**保证平安；这里得以保存，是因为人们尊重**自然**。这个项目证明，技术和保护并非对立面：只要谨慎使用，水泵和数据就能修复自然循环，而不是取代它。 那时，清晨的**露水**还在草上闪光。
