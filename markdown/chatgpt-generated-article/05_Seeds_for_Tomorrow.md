# 5. Seeds for Tomorrow（留给明天的种子）

**主题：** 自然地理 / 植物研究　 **目标词：** 41

Leila inherited an abandoned farm beside an old **petroleum** depot. The soil smelled of **gasoline**, and little **vegetation** remained. Neighbours advised her to sell, but her grandmother had once practised **horticulture** there and recorded every local **herb** in a **botany** notebook. Leila decided to **reclaim** the land and rebuild its **ecosystem** through **eco-friendly** methods. A laboratory confirmed that one oil-eating **fungus** could break down pollution without harming other **organisms**.

Leila used a **spade** and **plough** to loosen the **arable** field, then a **rake** to clear stones. She spread compost in a **heap**, stored straw in a **stack**, and tied seedlings into each **bundle**. In spring she began to **sow** grain and **cultivate** a **perennial** medicinal plant. She chose a **shade-tolerant** bean and a **short-day** flower whose **pollen** attracted native bees. Under the right conditions, the seeds began to **germinate**, each **bud** ready to **burgeon**.

Her brother, a geneticist, warned that pollution could cause a harmful **mutation**, so they monitored plant **genetics**, natural **variation**, and species **diversity**. Instead of careless **hybridisation**, they allowed selected plants to **reproduce** and **evolve** over several seasons. Their growth did **fluctuate**, and white **mould** ruined one harvest, but Leila did not quit. She also learned to **rear** chickens, whose manure enriched the soil. By the third year, workers could **pluck** herbs and gather a healthy **yield**. Measurements showed strong **photosynthesis** by day and the plants continued to **respire** normally at night. The depot owner finally paid for the cleanup. Leila kept her grandmother’s notebook in the farmhouse, now surrounded by green fields: proof that damaged land could recover when science served patience.

## 中文翻译

莱拉继承了一座废弃农场，旁边就是一处旧**石油**仓库。土壤散发着**汽油**气味，几乎没有**植被**存活。邻居劝她卖掉土地，但祖母过去曾在这里从事**园艺学**实践，还在一本**植物学**笔记里记录了当地每一种**药草**。莱拉决定**开垦修复**这片土地，用**环保的**方式重建它的**生态系统**。实验室证实，有一种能分解石油的**真菌**可以清除污染，又不会伤害其他**生物体**。

莱拉用**铲子**和**犁**翻松这块**可耕种的**田地，再用**耙子**清走石头。她把堆肥聚成一**堆**，把稻草垒成一**垛**，又将幼苗扎成一**捆**。春天，她开始**播种**谷物，并**栽培**一种**多年生的**药用植物。她挑选了**耐阴的**豆类和一种**短日照的**花，其**花粉**能吸引本地蜜蜂。条件适宜后，种子开始**发芽**，每个**芽**都即将**迅速生长**。

身为遗传学家的哥哥提醒她，污染可能引发有害**突变**，于是两人持续监测植物**遗传特征**、自然**变异**和物种**多样性**。他们没有草率进行**杂交**，而是让经过选择的植物自然**繁殖**，在多个季节里逐渐**进化**。植物生长确实会**波动**，白色**霉菌**也毁掉过一次收成，但莱拉没有放弃。她还学会**饲养**鸡，用鸡粪肥沃土壤。到了第三年，工人可以**采摘**药草，获得健康的**产量**。测量显示，植物白天进行旺盛的**光合作用**，夜间则正常**呼吸**。仓库所有者最终支付了清理费用。莱拉把祖母的笔记留在农舍里，四周如今全是绿色田野——它证明，只要科学与耐心同行，受损土地也能恢复。
