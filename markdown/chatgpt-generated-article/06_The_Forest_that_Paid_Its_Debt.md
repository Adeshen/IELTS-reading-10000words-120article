# 6. The Forest That Paid Its Debt（偿还债务的森林）

**主题：** 植物研究　 **目标词：** 41

Jin managed a failing **plantation** at the edge of a **rainforest**. For decades, workers had cut every useful **log** for **timber** and burned scraps into **charcoal**. The company’s cheerful tree **logo** could not hide dying soil, blocked water **circulation**, or rising carbon **emissions**. Jin, trained in **forestry**, promised the village that the land would no longer be exhausted for a quick profit.

He divided the slope into each **terrace** and planted **pine**, **olive**, and flowering **vine**. Along the river he restored **reed** beds; in open fields he protected the **meadow** and **lawn**. A living fence of **thorn** replaced wire. Beneath every broad **bough**, workers spread **hay** so that it could **decompose** rather than **rot** in wet piles. Scientists added an **enzyme** that acted as a **catalyst**, helping dead matter **decay** and **release** each **nutrient** into the soil. The new **mechanism** improved root **uptake** and helped **counterbalance** the carbon once lost.

The first year brought disappointment. Cut flowers became **stale**, young leaves **withered**, and algae—one green **alga** after another—filled an irrigation pond. Yet Jin reduced fertilizer before it could **exceed** safe levels and redirected water through the reeds. In spring, **violet** and **tulip** plants began to **bloom**. Their **aromatic** **scent** drifted over rows of **mint**; fruit trees **blossomed** and later **ripened**. By the fifth year, wildlife had returned and timber harvests came only from carefully selected trees. The plantation earned less in a single season but remained productive every year. At a village meeting, Jin removed the old logo from the gate. “A forest is not a warehouse,” he said. “Its real profit is that it can keep living.”

## 中文翻译

金管理着一座濒临倒闭的**种植园**，它位于**热带雨林**边缘。几十年来，工人把每根有用的**原木**都砍来制作**木材**，又把碎料烧成**木炭**。公司那个欢快的树形**标志**掩盖不了日渐衰败的土壤、受阻的水分**循环**以及不断增加的碳**排放**。受过**林业**训练的金向村民承诺，这片土地不会再为短期利润而被榨干。

他把山坡划成一道道**梯田**，种下**松树**、**橄榄树**和开花的**藤本植物**。他在河边恢复**芦苇**带，在开阔地保护**草甸**和**草坪**，还用带**刺**的活篱笆取代铁丝。工人把**干草**铺在每一根宽大**树枝**下，让它自然**分解**，而不是堆在潮湿处**腐烂**。科学家加入一种**酶**作为**催化剂**，帮助死亡物质**腐败分解**并向土壤**释放**各种**养分**。这套新**机制**提高了根系的**吸收**能力，也帮助**抵消**过去损失的碳。

第一年并不顺利。剪下的花变得**不新鲜的**，嫩叶逐渐**枯萎**，一株又一株绿色**藻类**塞满灌溉池塘。但金及时减少肥料，避免其浓度**超过**安全水平，并让水重新流经芦苇带。春天，**紫罗兰**和**郁金香**开始**盛开**，它们**芳香的****香气**飘过一排排**薄荷**；果树也开始**开花**，随后果实逐渐**成熟**。第五年，野生动物回来了，木材只取自经过谨慎选择的树木。种植园单季收入变少了，却能年年稳定生产。村民大会上，金取下大门上的旧标志：“森林不是仓库。它真正的收益，是能够一直活下去。”
