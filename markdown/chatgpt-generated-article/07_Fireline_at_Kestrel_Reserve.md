# 7. Fireline at Kestrel Reserve（红隼保护区的防火线）

**主题：** 植物研究 / 动物保护　 **目标词：** 41

When a **bush fire** began beyond Kestrel Reserve, **zoologist** Ava led a mixed team: an **ecologist**, a **botanist**, and local rangers. Their aim was not merely to **extinguish** flames but to stop the fire from causing the **extinction** of a rare frog **species**. This **amphibian** had **originated** in one isolated wetland; its survival now **derived** from a spring hidden among the reserve’s **flora**. If bulldozers entered carelessly, they might **demolish** nesting ground, **infringe** wildlife law, and **undermine** years of conservation.

The reserve held remarkable **fauna**: every **mammal**, **primate**, **vertebrate**, and **reptile** had a role in the ecosystem. A **herbivore** herd opened paths through grass, while each **carnivore** controlled weaker animals. As smoke thickened, a **flock** of birds fled above a **herd** of antelope. A **swarm** of insects moved like a dark cloud. Tourists formed a frightened **throng**, then a dangerous **crowd** at the only gate. One angry man called a trapped boar a **brute**, but Ava refused to leave it to **perish**.

Her team tested an **experimental**, **seasonal** firebreak planted with low-growing herbs. The conditions were **favourable**: it required **minimal** water, remained **productive** in drought, and provided **considerable** protection. Rangers used an **efficient** pump for **maximal** pressure and foam at the **optimal** concentration. A drone carried a emergency **destruct** command so it could safely destroy itself if flames damaged its controls. The method proved **effective**. Although the fire crossed an **immense** field and caused **massive** loss beyond the reserve, it stopped at the wetland. At dawn, Ava heard the rare frogs calling. The rescue did not erase the disaster, but it proved that careful science could protect both people and wildlife.

## 中文翻译

红隼保护区外发生**灌木丛火灾**后，**动物学家**艾娃带领一支联合队伍赶往现场，其中有一名**生态学家**、一名**植物学家**和几位当地护林员。他们的目标不只是**扑灭**火焰，更要阻止大火导致一种稀有青蛙**物种****灭绝**。这种**两栖动物**最初**起源于**一片孤立湿地；如今，它的生存**源自**保护区**植物群**之间的一眼隐秘泉水。如果推土机贸然进入，就可能**拆毁**筑巢地、**违反**野生动物法，并**削弱**多年的保护成果。

保护区拥有丰富的**动物群**，每一种**哺乳动物**、**灵长类动物**、**脊椎动物**和**爬行动物**都在生态系统中发挥作用。成**群**的**食草动物**踏开草间通道，各种**食肉动物**则淘汰虚弱个体。浓烟加重时，一**群**鸟从一**群**羚羊上方飞走，一**群**昆虫也像乌云般迁移。游客先聚成惊慌的**人群**，随后又在唯一出口形成危险的**拥挤人群**。一名愤怒男子把被困的野猪骂作**畜生**，艾娃却拒绝让它**丧生**。

团队测试了一道种有低矮草本植物的**实验性的**、适应**季节性的**防火带。当时条件十分**有利的**：它只需要**极少的**水，在旱季仍保持**多产的**状态，并能提供**相当大的**保护。护林员使用**高效的**水泵获得**最大的**压力，并把泡沫调到**最佳的**浓度。一架无人机装有小型**自毁**开关，若火焰损坏控制系统，便可安全自毁。这种方法很**有效的**。虽然大火越过一片**广阔的**田野，并在保护区外造成**巨大的**损失，却最终停在湿地前。黎明时，艾娃再次听见稀有青蛙鸣叫。救援无法抹去灾难，但它证明，严谨的科学能够同时保护人类与野生动物。
