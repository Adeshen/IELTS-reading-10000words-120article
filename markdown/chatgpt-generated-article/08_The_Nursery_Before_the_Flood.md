# 8. The Nursery Before the Flood（洪水来临前的育幼中心）

**主题：** 动物保护　 **目标词：** 41

At Riverbend Wildlife Centre, geneticist Mateo studied how every living **descendant** carried a thread from an ancient **ancestor**. A rescued wolf **cub**, an elephant **calf**, and the **offspring** of a rare **fox** formed one young-animal **subgroup** in the nursery. Nearby lived a **panda**, **kangaroo**, **camel**, **penguin**, **turtle**, and injured **whale**. The collection sounded improbable, but each animal had been seized from illegal traders and was waiting for a suitable home. The connection could **stem** from thousands of generations. The nursery also sheltered a rescued **wolf**.

Mateo’s job was to help threatened animals **breed** without damaging their identity. He would not let distinct species **interbreed** or **hybridize** merely to produce unusual attractions; such experiments could cause **sterility**. Instead, he recorded how adults selected a **mate**, performed **courtship**, guarded a **brood**, or returned to water to **spawn**. He watched each youngster **mature**, noting a soft **paw**, curved **beak**, damaged **fin**, healing **wing**, bright **plume**, stiff **bristle**, and even the **curl** of a tail. He also treated a **worm** **parasite** found near a web-building **spider**, while a **butterfly** and a noisy **cricket** occupied the insect house.

One night, floodwater broke through the outer wall. Mateo feared disease would **proliferate** if dirty water reached the nursery. He and keeper Suri moved the smallest animals first, then guided the **elephant** away from a display of confiscated **ivory**. A frightened **camel** kicked a gate with its **horn**-shaped handle, accidentally opening the route to higher ground. The centre’s Komodo **dragon** was carried in a secure crate, and the wolf followed its keeper. By dawn, no animal had been lost. Mateo later redesigned the centre around separate natural habitats. Visitors still came, but the nursery no longer displayed rescued creatures as curiosities; it prepared them to return, whenever possible, to lives of their own.

## 中文翻译

在河湾野生动物中心，遗传学家马特奥研究每一位活着的**后代**如何继承远古**祖先**留下的一条生命线。一只获救的狼**幼崽**、一头大象**幼兽**和一只稀有**狐狸**的**后代**组成了育幼中心的一个幼兽**亚组**。附近还住着**熊猫**、**袋鼠**、**骆驼**、**企鹅**、**海龟**和受伤的**鲸**。这些动物聚在一起看似不可思议，其实它们都从非法商贩手中被解救出来，正在等待合适的归宿。 这种联系可以**起源于**数千代以前。 育幼中心还收留了一只获救的**狼**。

马特奥的职责，是帮助濒危动物安全**繁殖**，又不破坏其物种特性。他绝不允许不同物种仅为了制造奇观而**异种交配**或进行**杂交**，因为这种实验可能导致**不育**。相反，他记录成年动物如何选择**配偶**、进行**求偶**、保护一窝**幼崽**，或回到水中**产卵**。他观察每只幼兽逐渐**成熟**，记录柔软的**爪**、弯曲的**喙**、受损的**鳍**、正在愈合的**翅膀**、鲜亮的**羽饰**、坚硬的**刚毛**，甚至尾巴的**卷曲**。他还治疗了一种**蠕虫****寄生虫**；这种虫出现在一只结网**蜘蛛**附近，而昆虫馆里还住着**蝴蝶**和吵闹的**蟋蟀**。

一天夜里，洪水冲破外墙。马特奥担心污水进入育幼区后疾病会迅速**增殖**。他和饲养员苏里先转移最小的动物，随后引导**大象**离开一处陈列被没收**象牙**的展柜。一头受惊的骆驼踢中了带有**角**形把手的门，意外打开了通往高地的路线。中心的科莫多**巨蜥**被装进安全箱，狼也跟随饲养员撤离。黎明时，没有一只动物失踪。后来，马特奥按照彼此独立的自然栖息地重新设计中心。游客依旧会来，但这里不再把获救动物当作奇观展示，而是帮助它们尽可能重返自己的生活。
