# 9. The Quarantine Barn（隔离谷仓）

**主题：** 动物保护　 **目标词：** 41

Veterinarian Hana arrived at Willow Farm after a newborn **lamb** developed a fever. The farm housed an **ox**, a breeding **bull**, two **buffalo**, a **horse**, **zebra**, and elderly **donkey**. In the **barn**, she removed the horse’s **saddle** and **harness**, then examined the lamb’s **anatomy**. A quick test revealed an unfamiliar **microbe**. If it caused an **epidemic**, every nearby animal **habitat** could be threatened.

Hana closed the gate in the thorny **hedge** and began tracing contact. A shepherd had recently found a fox **pup** beside a **hive**. Birds also moved freely through the yard: a **falcon** and **hawk** hunted above it; an **owl** nested in the roof; a **swallow**, **pigeon**, **crow**, **swan**, and **goose** drank from the same pond. Even the farm **cock** had escaped his pen. Near the grain store, a **mouse**, **rat**, **squirrel**, and **hare** could **creep** or **crawl** through small gaps. Any one of them might carry the **germ**.

Instead of blaming wildlife, Hana collected samples. She heard the bull **roar** when a bee delivered a painful **sting**, but the commotion led her to a leaking feed sack. Laboratory analysis showed that damp grain had grown the microbe. It blocked a **gene** involved in **metabolism**, preventing young animals from absorbing **protein** and a vital **vitamin**. Infected glands would **secrete** excess fluid, while the kidneys struggled to **excrete** waste.

Hana destroyed the spoiled feed, isolated the lamb, and disinfected the barn. Within two days its fever fell, and no other animal became ill. She also fitted mesh over storage vents without blocking the birds’ nesting sites. The farm reopened a week later. The lesson was simple: quarantine worked best when evidence, rather than fear, identified the true source of disease.

## 中文翻译

一只新生**羔羊**发烧后，兽医哈娜赶到柳树农场。农场里养着一头**阉牛**、一头种**公牛**、两头**水牛**、一匹**马**、一匹**斑马**和一头年迈的**驴**。她在**谷仓**里卸下马的**鞍**和**挽具**，随后检查羔羊的**解剖结构**。快速检测发现一种陌生**微生物**。如果它引发**流行病**，附近所有动物**栖息地**都可能受到威胁。

哈娜关上带刺**树篱**中的大门，开始追踪接触途径。一名牧羊人最近在一个**蜂箱**旁发现过狐狸**幼崽**。鸟类也会自由穿过院子：**隼**和**鹰**在上空捕猎，**猫头鹰**在屋顶筑巢，**燕子**、**鸽子**、**乌鸦**、**天鹅**和**鹅**则在同一池塘饮水。甚至农场的**公鸡**也逃出了围栏。粮仓附近，**小鼠**、**大鼠**、**松鼠**和**野兔**都能从狭小缝隙中悄悄**潜行**或**爬行**。它们中的任何一个都可能携带这种**病菌**。

哈娜没有责怪野生动物，而是采集样本。一只蜜蜂狠狠**蜇**了公牛，哈娜听见它发出**吼叫**，这场骚动却把她引向一个漏粮的饲料袋。化验显示，微生物来自受潮谷物。它会阻断一个参与**新陈代谢**的**基因**，使幼畜无法吸收**蛋白质**和一种关键**维生素**。受感染的腺体会**分泌**过量液体，肾脏也难以**排泄**废物。

哈娜销毁变质饲料，隔离羔羊，并给谷仓消毒。两天内，羔羊退烧了，没有其他动物患病。她还在通风口加装网罩，同时保留鸟类的筑巢位置。一周后，农场重新开放。事情说明：只有让证据而非恐惧找出疾病的真正来源，隔离措施才能发挥最佳效果。
