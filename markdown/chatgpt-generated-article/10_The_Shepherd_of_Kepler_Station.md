# 10. The Shepherd of Kepler Station（开普勒空间站的牧羊人）

**主题：** 动物保护 / 太空探索　 **目标词：** 41

Samir had once been a mountain **shepherd**; now he was the animal **keeper** aboard a research **spacecraft** crossing **interstellar** space. Its **propulsion** system carried a biological **module**, an astronomical **probe**, and seeds for a future **terrestrial** habitat. The crew called him “the shepherd of the **galaxy**,” although he knew the **cosmos** was too vast for anyone to herd. Their destination lay beneath a bright **celestial** object that **astronomy** identified as a planet, though an old sponsor had chosen it through **astrology**.

The module held insects, plants, and two small reptiles—not a display of **captive** life, but a test of whether a closed ecosystem could remain **functional**. One reptile was a **nocturnal** **predator** whose **instinct** was to **devour** moving **prey**; during simulated winter it became **dormant**, almost entering **hibernation**. Samir used **acoustic** and **optical** sensors to **trace** every movement. Their controls were **intuitive**, and **alternate** food chambers kept the **vulnerable** prey safe. The animals were not **tame**, so Samir wore **defensive** gloves whenever they **underwent** a health check.

Halfway through the voyage, a **meteorite** struck the outer shell. A **chunk** of metal tore loose, the station entered uncontrolled **motion**, and a broken **vent** sent cold air through the module. Flight **dynamics** predicted a collision with the planet’s moon and its enormous **crater**. The captain proposed emptying the animal chamber to save power, effectively choosing to **exterminate** its occupants. Samir argued that their survival was central to the mission, not a luxury. Using the reptiles’ heat-sensing **tail** cameras and the probe’s spare battery, he located the leak and sealed it.

The spacecraft regained control just as a **comet** crossed the distant sky. Months later, the ecosystem completed its trial without harming either predator or prey. Samir’s mountain skills—patience, observation, and respect for **subsistence**—had protected life between the stars.

## 中文翻译

萨米尔曾是一名山地**牧羊人**，如今却成了一艘穿越**星际的**太空研究**飞船**上的动物**饲养员**。飞船的**推进**系统载着一个生物**舱**、一枚天文**探测器**，以及未来建设**陆地的**栖息地所需的种子。船员称他为“**银河系**的牧羊人”，但他知道，**宇宙**辽阔得无人能够驱赶。目的地位于一个明亮**天体的**下方；**天文学**认定它是一颗行星，不过早期赞助人当初却是根据**占星术**选中的。

生物舱里有昆虫、植物和两只小型爬行动物。它们不是供人观赏的**被圈养的**生命，而是用于测试封闭生态系统能否保持**正常运转的**状态。其中一只是**夜间活动的****捕食者**，**本能**会**吞食**移动的**猎物**；模拟冬季到来时，它会进入**休眠的**状态，几乎开始**冬眠**。萨米尔用**声学的**和**光学的**传感器**追踪**每次活动。控制系统十分**直观的**，**交替的**食物舱则保护了**易受伤害的**猎物。这些动物并不**温顺的**，所以每当它们**经历**健康检查，萨米尔都会戴上**防护性的**手套。

航程过半时，一块**陨石**击中外壳。一大**块**金属脱落，空间站陷入失控**运动**，损坏的**通风口**又把冷空气送进生物舱。飞行**动力学**预测，他们将撞上行星的卫星和上面巨大的**陨石坑**。船长建议排空动物舱以节省能源，这实际上等于选择**消灭**其中的生命。萨米尔坚持认为，让它们生存下来是任务核心，而不是奢侈要求。他利用安装在爬行动物**尾巴**上的热感摄像头和探测器的备用电池找到泄漏点，将其封住。

飞船恢复控制时，一颗**彗星**正划过遥远天空。几个月后，生态系统顺利完成试验，捕食者和猎物都没有受到伤害。萨米尔从山区生活中学到的耐心、观察力，以及对**维持生计**之道的尊重，最终保护了星际之间的生命。
