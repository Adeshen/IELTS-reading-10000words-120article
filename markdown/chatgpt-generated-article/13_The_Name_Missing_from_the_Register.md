# 13. The Name Missing from the Register（名册上消失的名字）

**主题：** 学校教育　 **目标词：** 41

During **orientation**, history **fresher** Noor volunteered to help make a **documentary** about Bellweather University. The **principal**, the **dean**, and the entire history **faculty** wanted it ready for the **coed** institution’s centenary. Noor’s **mentor**, a retired **lecturer**, assigned a **sophomore** as her camera **assistant**. Their planned **coverage** included the **campus**, old **dorm**, **dining hall**, and chemistry **laboratory**. It sounded routine until Noor found that the first student **register** had a page cut out.

The missing page concerned Amara Vale, once a village **pupil** and later a respected **diplomat**. The official **roster** claimed she never completed **matriculation**, yet a newspaper **article** said she earned a **bachelor** degree there. Noor searched an **inventory** of boxes, an archive **index**, a handwritten **bibliography**, and an old **encyclopedia**. Inside a cracked picture **frame**, she discovered Amara’s **diary**, a campus **tale**, several pages of **poetry**, and a **sketch** of the original science building. Together they supplied the missing **context**: the university had once refused to **enrol** women openly, so Amara attended under her brother’s name.

The administrators feared the discovery would damage the centenary. Noor argued that hiding it would repeat the injustice. She prepared a balanced **profile** and showed her first **draft** to surviving **alumni**. A former registrar produced a procedure **manual** and Amara’s teaching **license**, both confirming the account. The dean agreed to **certify** the documents, while Noor’s mentor said the evidence would **qualify** for permanent archival status.

The finished documentary did not present the university as flawless. It showed how one determined student had entered secretly and helped later women study openly. At the premiere, Amara’s granddaughter placed the recovered name back on the register. The audience stood—not to celebrate a perfect past, but to honour a truth finally restored.

## 中文翻译

在新生**入学介绍**期间，历史专业**大一新生**努尔自愿协助拍摄一部介绍贝尔韦瑟大学的**纪录片**。**校长**、**学院院长**和整个历史系**全体教员**都希望影片赶上这所**男女同校的**大学百年庆典。努尔的**导师**是一名退休**讲师**，他给她安排了一名**大二学生**担任摄影**助理**。计划的报道**范围**包括**校园**、老**宿舍**、**食堂**和化学**实验室**。一切本来很寻常，直到努尔发现最早的学生**登记簿**被撕掉了一页。

缺失的一页涉及阿玛拉·韦尔。她曾是一名乡村**小学生**，后来成为受人尊敬的**外交官**。官方**花名册**声称她从未完成**大学入学手续**，一篇报纸**文章**却说她在那里获得了**学士**学位。努尔查阅箱子的**清单**、档案**索引**、手写**参考书目**和一本旧**百科全书**。她在裂开的相片**框**中发现阿玛拉的**日记**、一则校园**故事**、几页**诗歌**以及原科学楼的**素描**。这些材料共同补全了缺失的**背景信息**：学校当年不公开**招收**女性，所以阿玛拉借用哥哥的名字入学。

管理层担心发现会损害百年庆典。努尔认为，隐瞒真相只会重复当年的不公。她写出一份客观的生平**简介**，把第一份**草稿**交给仍健在的**校友**查看。一名前注册员拿出一本程序**手册**和阿玛拉的教师**执照**，两者都证实了这段经历。院长同意为文件**认证**，导师也认为证据**符合资格**，可以永久收入档案。

完成的纪录片没有把大学描绘成毫无瑕疵的机构。它讲述了一位坚定的学生如何秘密入学，又如何帮助后来的女性公开求学。首映式上，阿玛拉的孙女把失落的名字重新写回登记簿。全场观众起立致意——不是为了庆祝一个完美的过去，而是为了尊重终于得以恢复的真相。
