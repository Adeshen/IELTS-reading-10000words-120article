# 14. The Equation That Fed a Town（养活一座小镇的方程）

**主题：** 学校教育　 **目标词：** 41

Mei’s main **subject** was **engineering**, but she chose **agriculture** as a **minor** after floods isolated her hometown. When the university announced an interdisciplinary competition, she formed a team and wrote a **list** of the knowledge they needed: **geography** to map dry roads, **history** to locate old storehouses, **biology** and **chemistry** to protect food, **physics** and **mechanics** to repair vehicles, and **electronics** to track deliveries. No single textbook **volume** could solve the whole problem.

The team studied **economics**, **marketing**, and **accounting** because two struggling farm cooperatives were considering a **merger**. Mei conducted an **audit** and found that their combined **total** looked healthy only because both had recorded an **identical** shipment. She had to **subtract** it, leaving income **minus** transport costs. In **maths**, her model used **arithmetic**, **algebra**, and **calculus**. Demand became a **variable**, road capacity a **parameter**, and each delivery route an **equation**. After paying a small **dividend**, the remaining **remainder** had to fund emergency storage.

Professor Arun challenged the team’s **logic**. Their plan sent **double** the usual grain to one village, **triple** the milk to another, and **quadruple** the medicine to the clinic. An **odd** result was not necessarily wrong, Mei explained: populations and needs differed. Her decision was **rational**, not equal for appearance’s sake. Students in **sociology** interviewed families, while classmates in **philosophy** debated fairness. A **logistics** group then tested the route and found one bridge unsafe.

Mei redesigned the vehicle with help from **engineering**, **mechanics**, and **electronics** students, and the first supplies arrived before the next storm. A reporter filmed a **reel** of the convoy crossing the repaired road. The cooperatives completed their merger and adopted Mei’s transparent records. She had entered the competition hoping for a prize; she left understanding that disciplines mattered most when they worked together for people.

## 中文翻译

梅的主修**科目**是**工程学**，但洪水隔绝家乡后，她选择把**农业**作为**辅修科目**。大学宣布举办跨学科竞赛时，她组建团队，并列出一份所需知识**清单**：用**地理学**绘制仍然干燥的道路，用**历史学**寻找旧仓库，用**生物学**和**化学**保护食物，用**物理学**和**力学**修理车辆，再用**电子学**追踪运输。任何一本教材**卷册**都无法单独解决整个问题。

两家经营困难的农业合作社正在考虑**合并**，团队因此研究了**经济学**、**市场营销**和**会计学**。梅进行一次**审计**后发现，它们合计的**总数**之所以看起来不错，只因双方重复记录了一批**相同的**货物。她必须将其**减去**，再用收入**减去**运输成本。在**数学**模型中，她运用了**算术**、**代数**和**微积分**。需求是**变量**，道路容量是**参数**，每条运输路线则是一道**方程**。支付少量**股息**后，剩余的**余数**还要用于建设应急仓库。

阿伦教授质疑团队的**逻辑**。方案给一个村庄运送平时**两倍的**粮食，给另一个村庄送去**三倍的**牛奶，又给诊所提供**四倍的**药品。梅解释说，**奇数的**结果未必错误，因为人口与需求本来就不同。她的决定是**理性的**，而不是为了表面公平。**社会学**专业的学生采访家庭，**哲学**专业的同学讨论何为公正。随后，**物流学**小组测试路线，发现一座桥并不安全。

梅在工程、力学和电子学专业学生的协助下重新设计车辆，第一批物资终于在下一场暴风雨前抵达。记者拍摄了一**卷**车队驶过修复道路的影像。合作社完成合并，并采用梅制定的透明账目。她参加竞赛时只想获奖，离开时却明白：不同学科共同为人服务时，才能发挥最大的价值。
