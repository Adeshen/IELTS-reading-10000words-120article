# 15. The Purifier That Almost Failed（险些失败的净水器）

**主题：** 学校教育　 **目标词：** 41

For the school invention fair, Anya built a purifier from **multiple** chambers. On her work **table**, a **transparent** **rectangle** held a carbon **cube**, a **hollow** glass **sphere**, and a metal **cone**. A blue **dot** marked each inlet. She measured the **width**, drew a **diagonal** across the base, and used a **circle** to show the filter’s full **extent**. Her data **matrix** compared flow at every **scale**, from a **fraction** of an **ounce** to the pump’s **maximum** load.

Her **eccentric** science teacher insisted that precision mattered. Anya recorded each **decimal**, **percent**, **proportion**, and **ratio** of clean water to waste. She also checked liquid **density**, temperature in **Fahrenheit**, and the battery’s **volt** reading. The ultraviolet lamp would **radiate** energy and **emit** light to kill microbes. Carbon remained chemically **inert**, an **inherent** advantage because it would not **dissolve**, **melt**, or **rust** in water.

Minutes before judging, the pump began to shake. **Friction** heated a pipe, pressure caused water to **displace** trapped air, and a cracked thermometer released **mercury**. At the same time, fruit residue began to **ferment** and produced a **noxious** smell. A **static** spark could ignite the gas. Anya shut down the battery, opened the windows, and warned everyone to leave. She did not try to **dilute** the mercury or wash it away. Instead, the laboratory safety officer sealed the spill, while an **ozone** monitor confirmed that the air system was working.

The judges postponed the fair rather than disqualify her. Anya replaced the hot pipe, lowered the pump speed, and added a valve for trapped air. A week later, her purifier ran safely and produced the highest share of clean water. She won no prize for appearance, but received the engineering award for recognising failure, protecting others, and improving the design.

## 中文翻译

为了参加学校发明展，安雅制作了一台拥有**多个的**处理舱的净水器。工作**台**上，一个**透明的****长方形**容器里装着一块碳**立方体**、一个**中空的**玻璃**球体**和一个金属**圆锥体**。每个入口都标有一个蓝色**点**。她测量容器**宽度**，在底部画出一条**对角线**，再用一个**圆形**标明过滤区域的完整**范围**。数据**矩阵**比较了不同**尺度**下的流量，小到**几分之一****盎司**，大到水泵的**最大值**负荷。

她那位**古怪的**科学老师坚持认为，精确至关重要。安雅记录每个**小数**、**百分比**、**比例**和净水与废水的**比率**。她还检查液体**密度**、用**华氏度**表示的温度以及电池的**伏特**读数。紫外灯会**辐射**能量并**发出**光线，从而杀死微生物。碳在化学上保持**惰性的**，这是它的**固有的**优势，因为它不会在水里**溶解**、**熔化**或**生锈**。

距离评审开始只剩几分钟时，水泵突然震动。**摩擦**使管道升温，压力让水**排开**被困的空气，一个破裂的温度计还泄漏了**水银**。与此同时，水果残渣开始**发酵**，产生**有毒的**气味。一次**静电的**火花就可能点燃气体。安雅关闭电池、打开窗户，并提醒所有人撤离。她没有试图**稀释**水银或把它冲走，而是让实验室安全员封闭泄漏区域，同时用**臭氧**监测器确认空气系统仍在工作。

评委决定推迟展览，而没有取消她的资格。安雅换掉过热管道，降低水泵速度，又为受困空气增加阀门。一周后，净水器安全运行，产出的净水占比最高。它没有赢得外观奖，却获得了工程奖，因为安雅能够承认失败、保护他人并改进设计。
