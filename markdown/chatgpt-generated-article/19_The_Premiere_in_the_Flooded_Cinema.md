# 19. The Premiere in the Flooded Cinema（淹水影院里的首映）

**主题：** 科技发明 / 文化历史　 **目标词：** 40

Ivo promised to reopen the Orion Cinema for the town’s film **convention**. Its first programme would be a restored **science fiction** **epic** called *The Martian **Renaissance***, a film whose hopeful **ideology** had inspired his grandfather. Only one damaged **tape** survived. Ivo had to **convert** its analogue picture into **binary** data, restore the **audio**, and produce a **high-definition** image without trying to **alter** the director’s original **vision**.

The building presented a harder challenge. A cracked **loudspeaker**, rusty **microphone**, and broken projector **gear** were obvious. Less visible were blocked **drainage**, leaking **sewage**, and poor **ventilation**. Ivo installed a **wireless** sound link and an emergency **generator**. A **hydraulic** lift became the stage’s **pivot**, allowing scenery to **shift** between talks. His team built a **prototype** air system whose **filter** could **purify** incoming air, **compress** coolant, and **condense** moisture. During each testing **phase**, they would **refine** the design and **distil** complex readings into a simple safety report.

On opening night, a storm flooded the basement. One technician made a **wrong** connection and the projector entered maintenance **mode**. Ivo nearly **stumbled** over a cable while trying to **pinpoint** the **flaw**. His **contingency** plan saved the event: the generator powered pumps, and a second projector carried a **precise**, colour-**correct** copy of the film. Volunteers moved the audience upstairs while the new drainage system cleared the water.

The delay lasted twenty minutes. Instead of complaining, guests listened as Ivo explained how restoration could **facilitate** a meeting between past and present. When the repaired image finally appeared, scratches remained visible; he had preserved them deliberately as part of the film’s history. The audience applauded before the first scene ended. The Orion reopened not as a perfect imitation of its former self, but as an old building honestly adapted for a new generation.

## 中文翻译

伊沃答应在小镇电影**大会**前重新开放猎户座影院。首场节目是一部经过修复的**科幻**题材**史诗作品**《火星**文艺复兴**》，影片中充满希望的**思想观念**曾激励他的祖父。如今只剩下一盘受损**磁带**。伊沃必须把模拟画面**转换**成**二进制的**数据，修复**音频**，并生成**高清晰度的**图像，同时又不能**改变**导演原本的**构想**。

影院建筑更加棘手。破裂的**扬声器**、生锈的**麦克风**和损坏的放映机**齿轮**都很明显；不易察觉的问题则是堵塞的**排水系统**、渗漏的**污水**和糟糕的**通风**。伊沃安装了**无线的**声音连接和应急**发电机**。一座**液压的**升降台成为舞台的**枢轴**，让布景能在演讲之间**转换位置**。团队还制造一套空气系统**原型**，它的**过滤器**能够**净化**进入的空气、**压缩**制冷剂并**凝结**水分。每个测试**阶段**，他们都会继续**改进**设计，并把复杂读数**提炼**成简明的安全报告。

开幕当晚，暴风雨淹没了地下室。一名技术员接了一处**错误的**线路，放映机进入维护**模式**。伊沃寻找**精确定位**问题**缺陷**时，险些被电缆**绊倒**。他的**应急方案**挽救了活动：发电机为水泵供电，第二台放映机则播放一份色彩**准确的**、时间定位**精确的**影片副本。志愿者把观众转移到楼上，新排水系统则迅速排走积水。

放映只推迟了二十分钟。观众没有抱怨，而是听伊沃解释，修复如何**促进**过去与现在相遇。修复后的画面终于出现时，旧划痕仍清晰可见；他有意保留它们，因为那也是电影历史的一部分。第一个场景尚未结束，观众就响起掌声。猎户座影院并未成为旧日模样的完美复制品，而是以坦诚的方式，让古老建筑适应了新一代人。
