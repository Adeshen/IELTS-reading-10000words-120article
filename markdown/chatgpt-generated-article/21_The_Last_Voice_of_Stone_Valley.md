# 21. The Last Voice of Stone Valley（石谷最后的声音）

**主题：** 文化历史 / 语言演化　 **目标词：** 40

After years abroad, Mina grew **homesick** and returned to Stone Valley. Her grandmother was the last fluent speaker of its **dialect** and the unofficial **guardian** of its stories. Some recalled local **nobility**—a generous **lord** and a legendary **knight**—with **nostalgia**, but Grandmother said ordinary farmers had kept the valley alive through **adversity**. Their shared **experience**, not royal **background**, had allowed the community to **thrive** during brief years of **prosperity** and survive every **setback**.

A television **celebrity** arrived to record the “last speaker.” He treated each painted **symbol** as a picturesque **sign**, mistaking a **pictograph** of grain for decoration and a triangular **wedge** for a weapon. He even cut a ceremonial **knot** to display it flat. Mina stopped the filming. She explained that the language’s **oral** tradition could not be understood from objects alone. Her grandmother’s **handwriting** preserved only part of it; voice carried the rest. The archive would preserve every recorded **utterance**.

Mina, who studied **linguistics**, began a community **dictionary**. For every entry she recorded **semantic** meaning, **syntax**, and **grammar**, then described its **phonetics**. Children learned to **pronounce** each **syllable**, distinguish a **phoneme**, and hear how a **vowel** differed from a **consonant**. Mina marked changes in **intonation** and grammatical **inflection**, analysed each sentence **clause**, and explained how a **prefix** or **suffix** altered meaning. Where elders used a **logogram**, she kept the original mark rather than replacing it with an **abbreviation**.

The celebrity offered money for exclusive recordings, but Grandmother refused. Mina instead trained schoolchildren to interview elders and let every family keep a copy. Within a year, morning announcements included the valley language, and young musicians used it in songs. Grandmother was no longer presented as its final voice. She had become the first teacher of its next generation.

## 中文翻译

在国外生活多年后，米娜越来越**想家的**，于是回到石谷。祖母是当地**方言**最后一位流利使用者，也是故事的非正式**守护者**。有些人带着**怀旧之情**谈论本地昔日的**贵族**——一位慷慨的**领主**和一名传说中的**骑士**；祖母却说，是普通农民让山谷在**逆境**中延续下来。他们共同的**经历**而非王室**背景**，让族群在短暂的**繁荣**年代得以**兴旺发展**，也能挺过每一次**挫折**。

一位电视**名人**前来录制“最后的说话者”。他把每个彩绘**符号**当作好看的**标记**，误将表示谷物的**象形文字**视为装饰，又把三角形**楔形物**当成武器。他甚至剪开一个仪式用的**绳结**，好把它平放展示。米娜制止了拍摄。她解释说，仅凭物品无法理解这门语言的**口头的**传统。祖母的**笔迹**只能保留其中一部分，声音承载着其余部分。 档案还会保存每一段录下的**话语**。

学习**语言学**的米娜开始编写社区**词典**。她为每个词条记录**语义的**含义、**句法**和**语法**，再描述它的**语音学**特征。孩子们学习如何**发音**每个**音节**，辨别一个**音素**，并听出**元音**与**辅音**的区别。米娜标出**语调**和语法**屈折变化**，分析每个句子**从句**，并解释**前缀**或**后缀**如何改变意义。长者使用**语标文字**时，她保留原始符号，而不用**缩写**取代。

那位名人提出花钱购买录音独家使用权，祖母拒绝了。米娜转而训练学生采访长者，并让每个家庭都保留副本。一年之内，早间广播开始使用山谷语言，年轻音乐人也用它创作歌曲。祖母不再被包装成这门语言的最后声音；她成了下一代的第一位老师。
