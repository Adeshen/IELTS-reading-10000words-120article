# 22. The Interpreter Who Stopped a Strike（阻止罢工的口译员）

**主题：** 语言演化 / 娱乐运动　 **目标词：** 40

Mina was the youngest **correspondent** at a city newspaper when a dispute closed the harbour. The dockworkers **expected** the foreign owners to cut wages; management said that was only a **rumour**—a claim so **complicated** that even the official **commentary** confused readers. Mina noticed that the English **noun** “adjustment” had been rendered as the harsher local **verb** “reduce.” A **singular** error had spread through **plural** copies of a union **leaflet**.

The editor sent Mina as both reporter and **interpreter**. At the meeting, she wrote each **pronoun**, **adjective**, **adverb**, **preposition**, and **conjunction** on a board to show how the sentence worked. She supplied a neutral **synonym** for “adjustment” and its **antonym**, then **paraphrased** the whole clause. Her explanation **illuminated** what neither side had managed to **decipher**. She did not merely **translate**; she used calm **eloquence** to **narrate** how the mistake had travelled.

Arguments continued. A union **exponent** called the leaflet corporate **propaganda**, while a management **critic** accused the **press** of helping to **disseminate** panic. Mina proposed a **brainstorm** rather than another hostile **discussion**. The chief **negotiator** accepted her **handout**, and each side began to **communicate** its actual needs. Their chief **contention** proved to be overtime safety, not basic pay. Both sides then agreed to **negotiate** in good faith.

Outside, an **announcer** and a television **commentator** **awaited** news. Mina acted as **messenger**, carrying out the signed agreement and a corrected **pamphlet**. She had **anticipated** an angry headline; instead, her editor asked her to **compile** a guide showing journalists how to **interpret** labour documents. One accurately explained sentence had prevented a strike.

## 中文翻译

港口纠纷导致停工时，米娜还是市报最年轻的**通讯记者**。码头工人**预料**外资老板会削减工资；管理层却说那只是**传言**——声明十分**复杂**，连官方**评论**都把读者弄糊涂了。米娜发现，英语**名词**“调整”被译成了语气更重的本地语言**动词**“削减”。一个**单数形式的**错误，通过工会**传单**的**复数份数**迅速扩散。

编辑派米娜同时担任记者和**口译员**。会上，她把句中的每个**代词**、**形容词**、**副词**、**介词**和**连词**写在白板上，说明句子结构。她为“调整”提供了一个中性的**同义词**和一个**反义词**，又将整句话**换一种说法**。她的解释**阐明**了双方都没能**解读**清楚的问题。她不只是**翻译**，还以沉着的**口才**，**讲述**误译如何一步步传播。

争论仍在继续。一位工会观点的**倡导者**称传单是企业**宣传材料**，管理层的一名**批评者**则指责**新闻界**帮助**传播**恐慌。米娜建议大家进行一次**集思广益**，而不是再开一场充满敌意的**讨论**。首席**谈判代表**接受了她的**讲义**，双方开始**沟通**真正的需求。事实证明，他们争议的主要**焦点**是加班安全，而不是基本工资。 双方随后同意本着诚意继续**谈判**。

门外，一名**播音员**和一位电视**评论员**正在**等待**消息。米娜充当**传信人**，带出了签署完毕的协议和修正后的**小册子**。她原本**预想**会出现愤怒的头条，编辑却让她**编写**一份指南，教记者如何**理解**劳资文件。一个得到准确解释的句子，就这样阻止了一场罢工。
