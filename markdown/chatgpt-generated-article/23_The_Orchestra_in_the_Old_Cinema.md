# 23. The Orchestra in the Old Cinema（老电影院里的乐团）

**主题：** 娱乐运动　 **目标词：** 40

Restoring the abandoned cinema began as Leila's weekend **pastime**, a form of **recreation** shared with her grandfather, a retired **craftsman**. Soon **gossip** claimed that a developer would demolish it. Although the story was only a **rumour**, the neighbourhood reached a **consensus**: they would stage a benefit **concert** before the sale.

Leila designed a daring **programme**. A youth **orchestra** would perform a **classical** **symphony**, a **ballet** dancer would **improvise** during a **solo** **piano** passage, and local bands would mix **rock**, **hip-hop**, and **pop**. During **rehearsal**, a comic actor tried to **imitate** the conductor and **mimic** his stern **tone**. A screen behind them would **simulate** the building's former glory through **animation**, while a short **movie** would **depict** its history. Leila firmly rejected an **X-rated** poster offered as a joke; families, not shock, were the audience.

Every performer found a **role**. A singer rewrote a **lyric** to fit the old theatre's story, the brass section brightened the **melody**, and the drummer steadied its **rhythm** and **tune**. A travelling **circus** lent lights, and a retired actress turned a local **tragedy** into a brief **drama**. The event's **aesthetic** was deliberately simple: Leila chose to **opt** for handmade scenery and hung her grandfather's **portrait** in the foyer. Reviewers later struggled to **describe** its rough but honest beauty.

The final note was recorded on a **disc**, and the crowded hall fell silent. A wealthy arts **patron**, fascinated by the performance, offered to buy and preserve the cinema. Its sudden **popularity** saved it. Leila had not created perfect art, but she had shown what a community could become when it chose imagination over fear.

## 中文翻译

修复废弃电影院起初只是莱拉周末的**消遣**，也是她和退休**工匠**祖父共同参与的**娱乐活动**。不久，**闲谈**中传出开发商要拆楼的消息。虽然这只是**传言**，社区却达成了**共识**：他们要在出售前举办一场募款**音乐会**。

莱拉设计了一套大胆的**节目单**。青年**管弦乐团**将演奏一首**古典** **交响曲**，一名**芭蕾舞**演员会在**钢琴****独奏**段落中**即兴表演**，本地乐队则融合**摇滚乐**、**嘻哈乐**和**流行乐**。**彩排**时，一位喜剧演员试图**模仿**指挥的动作，并**仿效**他严厉的**语气**。身后的银幕通过**动画**来**模拟**建筑昔日的辉煌，一部短**电影**则**描绘**它的历史。莱拉坚决不用别人开玩笑送来的**限制级**海报；他们需要的是家庭观众，而不是靠惊吓吸引人。

每位表演者都有自己的**角色**。歌手重写一句**歌词**以配合影院的故事，铜管声部让**旋律**更加明亮，鼓手则稳住它的**节奏**和**曲调**。一个巡演**马戏团**借来了灯光，退休女演员还把当地一桩**悲剧**改成短小的**戏剧**。整场活动的**美学风格**刻意保持朴素：莱拉决定**选择**手工布景，并把祖父的**肖像**挂在门厅。评论者后来发现，那种粗粝而真诚的美很难**描述**。

最后一个音符被录进一张**光盘**，拥挤的大厅随即安静下来。一位富有的艺术**赞助人**被演出深深吸引，提出买下并保存影院。活动骤升的**人气**拯救了它。莱拉创造的艺术或许并不完美，却让人们看见：当一个社区选择想象力而不是恐惧时，它能够变成什么样子。
