# 24. One Last Lap for Nora（诺拉的最后一圈）

**主题：** 娱乐运动　 **目标词：** 40

At sixty-eight, Nora remained an **energetic** community coach, but a knee injury had ended her **vigorous** morning **cycling**. She still opened the **gym** each day, teaching gentle **indoor** **yoga** before leading **outdoor** **exercise**. When the council threatened to close the centre, she proposed a sports festival to prove its value.

The programme filled every corner. Retired players tried **chess** and **billiards**; children rotated through **badminton**, **tennis**, and **volleyball**. On the field, teams played **soccer** and **hockey**, sharing each **bat** and **racket**. Nora taught one child to **kick** without losing balance, another to **pitch** a ball, and a third to **throw** and **toss** safely. At the skating area, beginners learned to **glide**, **skate**, and **tumble** onto padded mats. A winter club even demonstrated how to **ski** on a short artificial slope. On Fridays, Nora also practised **golf** with her grandfather.

Local musicians supplied a **violin**, **cello**, **guitar**, **harmonica**, **trumpet**, and **flute** for an improvised parade. Their wealthy **patron**, however, promised funding only if Nora completed one lap of the cycling track. Spectators objected: the challenge might **strain** her injured knee. Nora refused to **sprawl** on the sidelines. She took time to **stretch**, climbed onto a modified bicycle, and began to pedal.

The reigning youth **champion** rode beside her, slowing whenever she slowed. At the last bend, Nora's wheel struck a cone and began to **flip**, but the champion steadied the bicycle before it could **knock** her down. Every **spectator** cheered as they crossed together. The patron honoured his promise, yet the greater victory was Nora's: she had shown that a sports centre was not built for champions alone, but for anyone willing to move, learn, and help another person finish.

## 中文翻译

六十八岁的诺拉仍是一位**精力充沛的**社区教练，但膝伤终止了她每日清晨的**高强度****骑行**。她依旧每天打开**健身房**，先教授舒缓的**室内****瑜伽**，再带大家做**户外****锻炼**。市政会威胁关闭中心时，她提议举办体育节，以证明这里的价值。

活动安排遍及每个角落。退休队员尝试**国际象棋**和**台球**；孩子们轮流参加**羽毛球**、**网球**和**排球**。运动场上，各队进行**足球**和**曲棍球**比赛，共用每一根**球棒**和每一只**球拍**。诺拉教一个孩子如何**踢球**而不失平衡，教另一个孩子**投球**，又让第三个孩子学会安全地**投掷**和轻轻**抛球**。在滑行区，初学者练习**滑行**、**滑冰**，并学会倒在垫子上安全地**翻滚**。一家冬季俱乐部甚至在短小的人造坡道上演示如何**滑雪**。 每逢周五，诺拉还会和祖父练习**高尔夫球**。

本地乐手拿来**小提琴**、**大提琴**、**吉他**、**口琴**、**小号**和**长笛**，组成一支即兴游行乐队。然而，他们富有的**赞助人**提出，只有诺拉完成自行车道一圈，他才提供经费。观众反对，因为挑战可能会**使她受伤**。诺拉不愿**摊开四肢躺在**场边。她认真**伸展**身体，骑上一辆改装自行车，开始踩踏板。

现任青年**冠军**陪她并肩骑行，每当她减速，他也跟着慢下来。最后一个弯道上，诺拉的车轮撞上路标，车身差点**翻倒**，冠军及时扶稳车子，没让它把她**撞倒**。两人一同冲线时，每位**观众**都在欢呼。赞助人兑现了承诺，但更大的胜利属于诺拉：她证明了体育中心不只为冠军而建，也属于每一个愿意行动、学习，并帮助别人抵达终点的人。
