# 25. The Clean Trail Challenge（洁净步道挑战）

**主题：** 娱乐运动 / 物品材料　 **目标词：** 40

During her **vacation**, Mei joined a mountain **excursion** advertised as a relaxed **hike** and **picnic**. The guide's long **stride** soon separated the group: some walkers began to **lag**, while others wanted to **linger** beside the river. At the next **step**, a distracted **pedestrian** slipped. His backpack took a wild **bounce** and began to **drift** toward a ravine until Mei made a quick **leap** and caught its strap.

The accident exposed a worse problem. Below them lay heaps of **garbage**, **rubbish**, **trash**, and broken camping **stuff**. Someone had dumped cheap **merchandise**, a cracked cooking **utensil**, and even a supposedly ancient **artifact** that proved to be a **crude** souvenir. Mei cancelled the planned **camp** and challenged everyone to clean the trail before sunset. They pitched one **tent** as a sorting station and used every spare bag in their emergency **kit**.

The hikers collected **litter** for **reuse**, separated useful objects from **junk**, and kept chemicals away from the stream. They found bottles of **detergent**, **lotion**, **shampoo**, and **soap**, plus a broken plastic **tub**. The guide explained that rain could carry these substances into the village **sewerage** system and eventually to a distant **landfill**. Cleaning was a **necessity**, not a pleasant extra. Everyone worked as if completing a **marathon**: they would **climb**, **jump**, and **plunge** their hands into thorny shrubs, then **toss** full bags up the slope.

The group missed its evening **cruise** but gained something better. Their matching cleanup **outfit** appeared in a local report, and the tour company promised to equip future hikers properly. At the trailhead, Mei looked back while sunlight seemed to **glide** across the restored river. Their holiday had become tiring, yet every careful step had returned a piece of the mountain to itself.

## 中文翻译

**假期**期间，梅参加了一次山区**短途旅行**，广告称那只是轻松的**徒步旅行**和**野餐**。导游迈着很大的**步幅**，很快拉开队伍：有人开始**落后**，有人则想在河边**逗留**。走到下一级**台阶**时，一名分心的**行人**滑倒了。他的背包猛地**弹起**，随后向峡谷边缘**漂移**；梅迅速**跳跃**过去，抓住了背带。

这次意外暴露了更严重的问题。下方堆满**垃圾**、**废物**、**垃圾杂物**和损坏的露营**物品**。有人丢弃了廉价**商品**、破裂的烹饪**器具**，甚至还有一件号称古董的**人工制品**，结果只是做工**粗糙的**纪念品。梅取消了原定的**露营**，提议所有人在日落前清理步道。他们支起一顶**帐篷**作为分类站，用上了应急**装备包**里的所有备用袋。

徒步者收集**散落垃圾**以便**再次利用**，把有用物件和**破烂**分开，并让化学品远离溪流。他们发现多瓶**洗涤剂**、**护肤液**、**洗发水**和**肥皂**，还有一个破裂的塑料**盆**。导游解释，雨水可能把这些物质带进村里的**污水处理系统**，最终流向远处的**垃圾填埋场**。清理是一项**必需之事**，不是可有可无的善举。大家像参加**马拉松**一样劳动：他们不断**攀爬**、**跳起**，把手**猛伸入**多刺的灌木，再把装满的袋子**抛向**坡顶。

团队错过了晚间**游船**，却获得了更珍贵的东西。他们统一的清洁**服装**出现在地方报道中，旅行公司也承诺为今后的游客提供妥善装备。回到步道入口，梅回头望去，阳光仿佛在恢复清澈的河面上**滑过**。这个假期很劳累，但每一个谨慎的脚步，都把一小片山野还给了自然。
