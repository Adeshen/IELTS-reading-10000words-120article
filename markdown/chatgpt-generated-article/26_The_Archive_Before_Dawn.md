# 26. The Archive Before Dawn（黎明前的档案室）

**主题：** 物品材料　 **目标词：** 40

On her first night as museum archivist, Sara heard water running. A pipe above the records room had burst near a **tap**, soaking a **rug**, a display **mattress**, and every protective **pad** and **sheet** beneath them. She pulled the electrical **plug**, checked the **fuse**, and kept water away from a loose **cable** and **cord**. Then she used a **sponge**, **towel**, **bucket**, and an old wooden **barrel** to control the leak.

The emergency threatened the museum's paper collection: a painter's **portfolio**, a rare **paperback**, a political **pamphlet**, and the only signed **memorandum** from the town's founder. Sara laid out dry **stationery**, a bottle of archival **ink**, and a block of **rubber** for making seals. She used a single **staple** only on modern copies, never on originals. Since the copier could not **Xerox** wet pages, she photographed each document and made a digital **duplicate**.

At midnight, smoke curled from the storeroom. One **strand** of wire had touched the metal **edge** of a cabinet. Sara struck a **match** to light a **candle** only after cutting the power, then opened a **shutter** and removed the cracked window **pane**. Its unusual **opacity** had hidden the smoke. With a **razor** and heavy **shears**, she cut damaged packing material from the **rim** of a case.

By dawn, each rescued object carried a numbered **tag** and accurate **label**. Sara wore her staff **badge** while a plumber tightened a **bolt** and replaced the broken **knob**. The director praised no single heroic **element**; preparation had been the decisive **factor**. Sara's careful choices, from switching off a fuse to recording every object, had saved a century of memory.

## 中文翻译

博物馆档案员萨拉上第一个夜班时，听见了流水声。档案室上方的一根水管在**水龙头**附近破裂，浸湿了**地毯**、展览用的**床垫**以及垫在下面的每块防护**垫**和**床单**。她拔掉电器**插头**，检查**保险丝**，让积水远离松脱的**电缆**和**电线**，再用**海绵**、**毛巾**、**水桶**和一个旧木**桶**控制漏水。

事故威胁着博物馆的纸质藏品：画家的**作品集**、珍贵的**平装书**、政治**小册子**，以及镇长留下的唯一一份亲笔**备忘录**。萨拉铺开干燥的**文具纸张**，拿出档案专用**墨水**和用于制印的**橡胶**块。她只用一枚**订书钉**固定现代复印件，绝不碰原件。复印机无法**复印**湿纸，她便逐页拍照，制作电子**副本**。

午夜时，储藏室冒出烟。一**股**电线碰到了柜子的金属**边缘**。萨拉切断电源后，才划亮一根**火柴**点燃**蜡烛**，随后打开**百叶窗**，取下开裂的窗**玻璃**。玻璃异常的**不透明性**此前遮住了烟雾。她用**剃刀**和结实的**大剪刀**，剪掉展柜**边框**上的受损包装材料。

黎明前，每件获救藏品都挂上编号**吊牌**和准确的**标签**。萨拉戴着员工**徽章**，看着水管工拧紧**螺栓**并更换损坏的**旋钮**。馆长没有把功劳归给某个英雄式的**要素**；真正决定成败的**因素**是充分准备。萨拉从关闭保险丝到登记每件藏品的一系列谨慎选择，保住了一个世纪的记忆。
