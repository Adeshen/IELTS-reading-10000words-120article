# 27. The Bellmaker's Last Order（铸钟匠的最后一份订单）

**主题：** 物品材料　 **目标词：** 40

Elias ran an old **forge** beside a water **mill**. When the town commissioned a memorial **bell**, he promised his late father that he would cast it by hand. He first used an iron **pail** to carry sand from the casting **pit**. On a high **shelf**, he arranged samples of **metal**—**iron**, **brass**, **bronze**, and a new **alloy**—while his daughter Mara recorded each trial with a **camera** under a bright **spotlight**.

The first casting failed. A broken **bulb** hid a crack, and the lifting **ladder** caught on a **hurdle** beside the **fence**. Elias sat on a wooden **stool**, ready to quit. Mara opened a **drawer** containing his father's notes. They explained how to **lubricate** the mould's pedal mechanism and balance the furnace with a **portable** air pump. After clearing dust with a **vacuum**, they tried again. A small **fridge** stood beside the workshop sink.

They built the mould from **lumber**, **cement**, **lime**, and **plaster**. Mara stretched **canvas** around it and covered nearby tools with **plastic**. For protective clothing, neighbours helped **weave** strong **linen** and **cotton** **fabric**, then **knit** cuffs reinforced with **nylon**. A storm began, so everyone arrived carrying an **umbrella** or wearing a **raincoat**. Even the harbour crew lent a small **dredge** to remove mud blocking the millrace.

Near dawn, the molten bronze filled the mould. Mara pressed the **pedal**, and the machinery held steady. When they finally raised the bell, morning light caught a tiny **crystal** embedded in its surface. Elias chose not to polish it away. At the dedication, the bell's clear note crossed the valley. The flaw did not weaken the sound; it made the town's last handmade bell unmistakably its own.

## 中文翻译

埃利亚斯在水力**磨坊**旁经营一间老**锻造坊**。镇上委托他制作一口纪念**钟**时，他向已故父亲承诺要亲手铸造。他先用铁**桶**从铸造**坑**搬沙。在高高的**架子**上，他排列好**金属**样品——**铁**、**黄铜**、**青铜**和一种新**合金**；女儿玛拉则在明亮的**聚光灯**下用**照相机**记录每次试验。

第一次铸造失败了。损坏的**灯泡**掩盖了一条裂纹，吊装用的**梯子**又卡在**围栏**旁的**障碍物**上。埃利亚斯坐在木**凳子**上，几乎想放弃。玛拉打开一个**抽屉**，找到了祖父的笔记。上面说明了如何给模具的踏板装置**加润滑油**，以及如何用一台**便携式**气泵平衡炉火。他们用**吸尘器**清除灰尘后，再次尝试。 工坊水槽旁还放着一台小**冰箱**。

他们用**木材**、**水泥**、**石灰**和**灰泥**制作模具。玛拉在外面绷紧**帆布**，并用**塑料**遮住附近工具。为了制作防护服，邻居们帮忙**编织**结实的**亚麻**和**棉**质**织物**，又为袖口加上**针织**的**尼龙**加固层。暴风雨开始后，人人都拿着**雨伞**或穿着**雨衣**赶来。就连港口人员也借来一艘小型**挖泥船**，清除磨坊水道里的淤泥。

接近黎明时，熔化的青铜注满模具。玛拉踩下**踏板**，机器平稳运行。钟被吊起时，晨光照亮了表面嵌着的一小块**水晶**。埃利亚斯决定不把它磨掉。落成仪式上，清澈的钟声传遍山谷。那处瑕疵没有削弱声音，反而让镇上最后一口手工钟独一无二。
