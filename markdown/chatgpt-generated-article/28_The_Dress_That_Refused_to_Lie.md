# 28. The Dress That Refused to Lie（不肯说谎的礼服）

**主题：** 物品材料 / 时尚潮流　 **目标词：** 40

Young designer Anika was invited to a **luxury** **fashion** show whose **popularity** could launch her career. The current **vogue** favoured **extravagant** gowns, so the sponsor demanded that she **adorn** her model with **jewellery**, a large **gem**, and carved **jade**. He also supplied an “**exquisite**” imported **pigment** and **dye**. Anika soon discovered that every item was **counterfeit**: the stones were **fake**, the cloth **inferior**, and the colour unsafe.

The sponsor insisted that appearances should **prevail**. He wanted her to **embellish** the lie, **embroider** his logo across the skirt, and hide the model's face beneath a **veil**. “People want an **icon**, not a lecture,” he said, pointing to a famous **idol** on the poster. Anika knew the publicity was **available** to her only if she obeyed. Instead, she turned the evening into a **masquerade** of truth.

She created a **miniature** version of the false gown and displayed it beside laboratory results. Her real **costume** was a **decent**, **durable** **dress** made by local workers. Its only **ornament** was recycled glass, and careful **hairdressing** replaced expensive **cosmetics**. The model, a **handsome** dancer with natural **grace** and **elegance**, looked both **charming** and **pretty** without pretending to be someone else. The sponsor called the honest design **ugly**. After the exhibition, she returned the restored dress to the museum **wardrobe**.

For a moment the hall was silent. Then buyers began photographing the labels and asking where the local dress would be sold. Its **overall** simplicity **fascinated** them more than the glittering fraud. Anika lost her sponsor but won orders from ethical shops. By refusing to decorate a lie, she began a fashion career she could be proud to wear.

## 中文翻译

年轻设计师安妮卡受邀参加一场**奢华的****时装**秀；这场活动的**人气**足以开启她的事业。当时的**流行风尚**偏爱**铺张的**礼服，赞助人便要求她用**珠宝首饰**、一颗大**宝石**和雕刻**玉石**来**装饰**模特。他还提供了号称“**精美的**”进口**颜料**和**染料**。安妮卡很快发现所有物品都是**仿冒品**：宝石是**假的**，布料**劣质**，颜色也不安全。

赞助人坚持让外表**占上风**。他要她**美化**谎言，在裙子上**刺绣**自己的标志，并用**面纱**遮住模特的脸。“人们想看的是**偶像人物**，不是听课。”他说着，指向海报上的著名**偶像**。安妮卡明白，只有服从，自己才能获得这些**可得到的**宣传机会。她却决定把当晚变成一场揭露真相的**化装舞会**。

她制作了一件假礼服的**微型**版本，把它与实验报告并列展出。她真正的**演出服**是一件由本地工人制作、**得体**而**耐用的****连衣裙**。唯一的**饰品**是再生玻璃，精心设计的**发型**取代了昂贵**化妆品**。模特是一位**英俊的**舞者，带着天生的**风度**与**优雅**；无须假扮别人，他依然显得**迷人**而**漂亮**。赞助人却骂这件诚实的设计**丑陋**。 展览结束后，她把修复好的裙子送回博物馆的**衣柜**。

大厅一时寂静无声。随后，买家纷纷拍摄标签，询问哪里能买到这件本地礼服。它**整体上的**简洁比闪亮的骗局更令他们**着迷**。安妮卡失去了赞助人，却赢得了重视道德的商店订单。因为拒绝装点谎言，她开启了一份真正可以自豪地“穿在身上”的时尚事业。
