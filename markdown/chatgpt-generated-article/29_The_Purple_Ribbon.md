# 29. The Purple Ribbon（紫色缎带）

**主题：** 时尚潮流　 **目标词：** 40

Before the winter pageant, costume assistant Lian discovered that the leading actress's **gown** had vanished. In its place lay a torn **robe**, a **brown** **sweater**, and a note demanding money. The thief had left muddy **stains** on a **white** **jacket** and a dark **blot** on a **yellow** **skirt**. A **slender** thread of **purple** **velvet** caught in the door's **clasp** gave Lian her first clue.

She followed scraps past the dressing rooms: a **pink** **ribbon** near a **wardrobe**, one glass **bead** beside a **purse**, and a bent **pin** under a **hat** with a wide **brim**. Backstage, an anxious dancer wore **jeans**, while the stage manager pulled on **trousers**, one **sock**, and a single **slipper** in his rush. Nobody had seen the gown, although a guard remembered someone hiding beneath a **cloak**, with a **scarf** wrapped above its **collar**.

Lian found the missing costume in the laundry, not with a criminal. An elderly tailor had taken it after its shoulder **strap** snapped. He had added a hidden **patch**, repaired every **stitch**, replaced torn **lace**, and attached a new silver **bracelet** as decoration. The suspicious velvet thread came from his worktable; the ransom note was merely a prop from another play. His **glove**, **cap**, and **tan** apron had made him look like the cloaked stranger on a dim security camera.

There was barely time to **wrap** the actress in her costume. Lian tightened a loose **stripe** of **textile**, fastened the gown, and sent her onstage. The dress fitted perfectly—not too **tight**—and the repaired shoulder held. After the applause, Lian apologised to the tailor. He gave her the purple ribbon, reminding her that evidence must be sewn into a whole story before anyone is accused.

## 中文翻译

冬季庆典开始前，服装助理莲发现女主角的**礼服**不见了。原处只剩一件破损的**长袍**、一件**棕色****毛衣**和一张索要钱财的纸条。小偷在一件**白色****夹克**上留下泥**渍**，又在一条**黄色****裙子**上留下深色**污点**。门的**搭扣**上挂着一根**纤细的****紫色****天鹅绒**线，成为莲的第一条线索。

她沿着碎屑走过化妆间：**衣柜**旁有一条**粉色****缎带**，**手提包**旁有一颗玻璃**珠子**，宽**帽檐**的**帽子**下面还有一枚弯曲的**别针**。后台，一名焦急的舞者穿着**牛仔裤**，舞台经理匆忙间套上**长裤**、一只**袜子**和一只**拖鞋**。没有人见过礼服，不过保安记得有人藏在**斗篷**下，并用**围巾**遮住了**衣领**上方。

莲最终在洗衣房找到了礼服，但那里没有罪犯。一位老裁缝发现礼服肩部**带子**断裂，便把它拿走修补。他加上隐蔽的**补丁**，修好每一道**针脚**，换掉撕裂的**蕾丝**，还系上一只新银**手镯**作为装饰。可疑的天鹅绒线来自他的工作台；勒索信其实只是另一出戏的道具。他的**手套**、**便帽**和**棕褐色**围裙，让昏暗监控中的他看起来像那个披斗篷的陌生人。

时间只够把服装迅速**裹在**女演员身上。莲拉紧一条松脱的**条纹****布料**，扣好礼服，送她登台。衣服十分合身，并不太**紧**，修好的肩部也稳稳撑住。掌声过后，莲向裁缝道歉。老人把紫色缎带送给她，提醒她：证据必须先缝合成完整的故事，才能指控任何人。
