# 30. Supper for an Unexpected Guest（为不速之客准备的晚餐）

**主题：** 时尚潮流 / 饮食健康　 **目标词：** 40

**Chef** Omar had to **cater** a diplomatic **banquet** in the college **refectory**, but a storm delayed the food truck. His remaining **provisions** were **slight**: one **edible** mushroom basket, vegetables, and an old family **recipe**. Worse, the guest of honour was a **vegetarian** **gourmet** with little **appetite** after travelling.

Omar rejected the easy option of ordering **takeaway**. He prepared a small **appetiser**, then turned the mushrooms into a rich main **dish** and baked a fruit **treat** for **supper**. Students arranged every **tray** with **silver** **cutlery**: a **fork**, **spoon**, and polished knife. They borrowed a **porcelain** **bowl**, **ceramic** **saucer**, and sturdy **mug** from the art department. In the kitchen, a cracked **pot** stood near the old **furnace**; Omar moved it to the **oven**, covered it with a **lid**, and let the sauce deepen.

For **refreshment**, he served fruit **juice**, **soda**, and **coffee**. The organiser had requested **liquor**, including **whisky** and **brandy**, but Omar discovered that the guest avoided alcohol and **tobacco**. He replaced both with a fragrant spiced **beverage**. A hungry guard received a quick **snack** while everyone waited.

The guest arrived wearing an elegant **overall** beneath a raincoat and smiled at the modest table. She tasted the meal slowly, then asked for the recipe. Omar admitted how close the banquet had come to failure. “Good hospitality is not measured by abundance,” she said. “It is measured by attention.” Her praise restored the exhausted chef's appetite, and the storm-bound college shared the finest simple supper it could make.

## 中文翻译

**厨师**奥马尔要在学院**食堂**为一场外交**宴会****承办餐饮**，暴风雨却延误了食品车。他剩下的**食物储备**十分**少**：只有一篮**可食用的**蘑菇、一些蔬菜和一道祖传**食谱**。更糟的是，主宾是一位旅途后毫无**食欲**的**素食者**兼**美食家**。

奥马尔没有选择简单订购**外卖**。他先做了一小份**开胃菜**，再把蘑菇制成浓郁的主**菜**，并为**晚餐**烤制水果**甜点**。学生把每个**托盘**都摆上**银制****餐具**：一把**叉子**、一把**勺子**和擦亮的餐刀。他们从艺术系借来一只**瓷制****碗**、一个**陶制****茶碟**和结实的**马克杯**。厨房里，一口裂开的**锅**靠近旧**炉子**；奥马尔把食物移入**烤箱**，盖上**盖子**，让酱汁逐渐浓郁。

作为**茶点饮料**，他准备了水果**果汁**、**汽水**和**咖啡**。组织者原本要求提供**烈酒**，包括**威士忌**和**白兰地**，奥马尔却发现客人不饮酒，也远离**烟草**。他便以芬芳的香料**饮料**代替。等待期间，一名饥饿的保安还得到了一份快捷**小吃**。

客人到达时，雨衣下面穿着优雅的**罩衣**。她看着朴素餐桌微笑，慢慢品尝食物，随后索要食谱。奥马尔坦言宴会差点失败。“真正的待客之道不以丰盛衡量，”她说，“而在于用心。”她的赞美也恢复了疲惫厨师的食欲；被暴风雨困住的学院，共享了他们所能做出的最美好简餐。
