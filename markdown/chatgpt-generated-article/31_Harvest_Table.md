# 31. The Harvest Table（丰收长桌）

**主题：** 饮食健康　 **目标词：** 40

When floodwater isolated Greenbank Farm, twelve schoolchildren were stranded there overnight. Farmer Rosa had expected to **reap** **wheat** and **corn**, not feed a class, yet she refused to let anyone go hungry. She showed them the **livestock**, then explained that the **dairy** supplied **milk**, **cheese**, and **butter**, while meat such as **beef**, **pork**, and **sausage** would remain frozen for another day.

The children turned dinner into a rescue project. One team harvested **tomato**, **cabbage**, **turnip**, and **radish** for **soup** and **salad**. They learned to **peel** roots, **strip** damaged leaves, and remove the **hull** from grain. Beside the **pond**, Rosa used a fishing **rod** to catch nothing at all, so everyone laughed and returned to the kitchen.

Another team sorted fruit: **cherry**, **berry**, **grape**, **papaya**, **pear**, **plum**, **orange**, **melon**, and **kiwi**. They crushed the soft pieces into a sweet **paste** for **pudding**, while firmer slices topped a **pie**. The youngest children spread butter on each **biscuit** and built a giant **sandwich**. Older pupils made **pasta** and **spaghetti**, then shaped spare dough into a simple **pizza**. One boy requested a **hamburger**, but cheerfully accepted the vegetable feast instead.

By evening, the flood had not fallen, but fear had. The children carried every dish to one long table in the barn. Rosa worried that the meal looked untidy; their teacher saw something else—a harvest shared fairly, with no ingredient wasted. When rescuers arrived, the children asked to finish dinner first. They left the next morning knowing that a farm was more than a place that produced food: in a crisis, it could become a generous home.

## 中文翻译

洪水把绿岸农场与外界隔绝时，十二名小学生被困在那里过夜。农场主罗莎原本准备**收割****小麦**和**玉米**，没想到要招待一个班，但她不肯让任何人挨饿。她带孩子们参观**牲畜**，并说明**乳品场**提供**牛奶**、**奶酪**和**黄油**；至于**牛肉**、**猪肉**和**香肠**等肉类，则留在冷柜里以后再吃。

孩子们把准备晚餐变成了一项自救任务。一队采摘**番茄**、**卷心菜**、**芜菁**和**萝卜**，用来做**汤**和**沙拉**。他们学着给根茎**削皮**、**剥去**坏叶，并去掉谷物的**外壳**。在**池塘**边，罗莎用钓鱼**竿**尝试捕鱼，却一条也没钓到；大家笑过之后又回到厨房。

另一队整理水果：**樱桃**、**浆果**、**葡萄**、**木瓜**、**梨**、**李子**、**橙子**、**甜瓜**和**猕猴桃**。他们把柔软的果肉压成甜**糊**，用来制作**布丁**，较硬的果片则铺在**馅饼**上。年纪最小的孩子在每块**饼干**上抹黄油，又做了一个巨大的**三明治**。大孩子制作**意大利面食**和**细面条**，再把剩余面团做成简易**比萨饼**。一个男孩想吃**汉堡包**，但最后也开心地接受了蔬菜大餐。

夜幕降临时，洪水没有退，恐惧却消失了。孩子们把每道菜端到谷仓里的一张长桌上。罗莎担心饭菜看起来杂乱；老师看到的却是另一番景象——收获被公平分享，没有任何食材遭到浪费。救援人员到达时，孩子们请求先吃完晚餐。第二天早晨离开时，他们明白了：农场不只是生产食物的地方；危急时刻，它也能成为一个慷慨的家。
