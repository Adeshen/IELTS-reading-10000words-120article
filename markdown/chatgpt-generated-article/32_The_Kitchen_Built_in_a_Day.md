# 32. The Kitchen Built in a Day（一天建成的厨房）

**主题：** 饮食健康 / 建筑场所　 **目标词：** 40

Maya planned to **establish** a community kitchen beside the town's oldest **landmark**. On opening morning, an inspector declared the temporary **structure** unsafe: workers had begun its **erection** on a soft **site**, and stacked boxes **obstructed** the exit. Maya had one day to **construct** a stronger frame. Volunteers brought **steel**, tested its **stability**, and moved the festival to a nearby **venue** while repairs continued.

To keep the crowd cheerful, Maya invited children to **cook**. They mixed **chocolate**, **vanilla**, **sugar**, and a pinch of **salt**, then **stirred** the batter and began to **bake**. For lunch, adults prepared **ginger**, **scallion**, **mustard**, **wasabi**, and every fragrant **spice** they could find. Each **ingredient** went into a labelled bowl. One team made tomato **sauce** and **ketchup**; another let beans **soak** before cooking.

The tasting table offered **sweet**, **sour**, **bitter**, and **spicy** dishes. A child called the noodles **yummy**, then became **thirsty** and asked for water. Another carefully **licked** melting **ice cream** from a spoon, choosing **chocolate** over **vanilla**. Maya cut each cake **slice** evenly and handed out fruit **candy** and mint **gum**. She explained that vivid **flavour** did not require too much sugar and that a vitamin **supplement** could not replace food the body could properly **digest**.

At sunset, the inspector returned. The exit was clear, the steel frame secure, and the kitchen ready. Maya expected praise for the building, but he pointed to the crowded tables instead. The real landmark, he said, was not the new structure; it was the trust created when neighbours solved a problem and shared a meal.

## 中文翻译

玛雅计划在镇上最古老的**地标**旁**建立**一间社区厨房。开业当天早晨，检查员判定临时**结构**不安全：工人把它的**搭建**地点选在松软的**场地**上，堆放的箱子还**阻塞**了出口。玛雅只有一天时间**建造**更牢固的框架。志愿者运来**钢材**，检测它的**稳定性**，并在维修期间把节庆活动转移到附近的**场馆**。

为让人群保持愉快，玛雅邀请孩子们一起**烹饪**。他们混合**巧克力**、**香草**、**糖**和少量**盐**，再**搅拌**面糊，开始**烘烤**。午餐时，大人们准备**姜**、**葱**、**芥末**、**山葵**以及能找到的各种芳香**香料**。每种**原料**都放进贴有标签的碗里。一组制作番茄**调味汁**和**番茄酱**，另一组则先把豆子**浸泡**再煮。

品尝桌上有**甜的**、**酸的**、**苦的**和**辛辣的**菜肴。一个孩子说面条很**美味**，随后觉得**口渴**，便要了一杯水。另一个孩子小心地**舔着**勺子上融化的**冰激凌**，选择了**巧克力**而不是**香草**口味。玛雅把蛋糕的每一**片**切得一样大，又分发水果**糖果**和薄荷**口香糖**。她解释，鲜明的**味道**不需要过量糖分，维生素**补充剂**也不能代替身体能够正常**消化**的食物。

日落时，检查员回来了。出口畅通，钢架稳固，厨房已经准备就绪。玛雅以为他会称赞建筑，他却指向坐满人的餐桌。他说，真正的地标并不是新建筑，而是邻居们共同解决问题、分享食物时建立的信任。
