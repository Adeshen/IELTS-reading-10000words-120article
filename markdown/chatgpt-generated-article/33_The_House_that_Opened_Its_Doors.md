# 33. The House that Opened Its Doors（敞开大门的老宅）

**主题：** 建筑场所　 **目标词：** 40

After an earthquake forced hundreds of families to **migrate** from the mountains, architect Mei Chen was asked to **locate** a safe temporary home for them. She chose Alder House, an empty **mansion** **situated** beyond the city's last **skyscraper**. It had once been a merchant's **villa**, then a youth **hostel**, while the gatekeeper still continued to **reside** in a tiny **lodge**. Mei hoped that twenty families could **inhabit** the building until each received a permanent **dwelling**. Others would use a wooden **cabin** and a garden **shed** as classrooms. The house, local people said, was the **cradle** of the town's first public library.

Mei mapped every room on a square **grid**. From the stone **porch**, a wide **aisle** led across the **lobby** to a long **corridor**. One branch ended at the **living room** and the old **parlour**, where a cracked **fireplace** faced a cold **radiator**. Another led to the **kitchen**, its empty **cabinet**, a small **lavatory**, and a separate **toilet**. Upstairs, she tested each **stair** of the main **staircase**, gripping the loose **handrail**. There was no **escalator**, of course, but the children laughed when the caretaker described the moving stairs in the city mall.

That evening, rain entered beneath the **eaves** and soaked a damaged roof **beam**. Water ran down a marble **pillar** and a wooden **column**, then through a thin **layer** of plaster. When part of the ceiling fell, it blocked the passage to the **cellar**, where medicine had been stored beneath a brick **vault**. Mei crawled through a narrow **arch** and found that the beam beside the **chimney** was beginning to split. If it failed, the whole western wing could collapse while the refugees slept.

Mei cancelled the move-in and called local carpenters. They reinforced the beam, repaired the roof, and reopened the cellar before dawn. Two days later, the families entered safely. Children filled the former parlour with books, and meals steamed in the kitchen again. Alder House had been built for one wealthy family, but in its most important season, it became a home for many.

## 中文翻译

地震迫使数百户家庭从山区**迁移**后，建筑师陈梅受命**寻找**一个安全的临时住所。她选中了奥尔德老宅；这座空置的**宅邸****坐落于**城市最后一栋**摩天大楼**之外。它最初是商人的**别墅**，后来改成青年**旅舍**，守门人则仍然**居住**在一间小**门房**里。梅希望二十户家庭能够暂时**居住在**楼内，直至每户都获得永久**住所**。花园中的木制**小屋**和**棚屋**则可作为教室。当地人说，这栋房子还是本镇第一所公共图书馆的**发源地**。

梅用方形**网格**标出了每个房间。从石砌**门廊**进入后，一条宽阔的**通道**穿过**门厅**，连接一条长长的**走廊**。其中一个分支通往**客厅**和旧日的**会客室**，那里一座开裂的**壁炉**正对着冰冷的**暖气片**。另一条路通向**厨房**、空置的**橱柜**、一间小**盥洗室**以及独立的**厕所**。上楼时，她紧握松动的**扶手**，逐一检查主**楼梯**上的每一级**台阶**。这里当然没有**自动扶梯**，但当管理员描述城市商场里会移动的楼梯时，孩子们都笑了起来。

当天傍晚，雨水从**屋檐**下渗入，浸湿了一根受损的屋顶**横梁**。水沿着一根大理石**支柱**和一根木制**立柱**流下，又穿过薄薄一**层**灰泥。部分天花板坠落后，通往**地窖**的道路被堵住；药品就存放在地窖的砖砌**拱顶**下。梅从一道狭窄的**拱门**爬过去，发现**烟囱**旁的横梁正在开裂。如果它断掉，难民睡觉时整座西翼都可能坍塌。

梅取消了当晚的入住安排，并召集当地木匠。他们加固横梁、修复屋顶，在黎明前重新打通了地窖。两天后，各户家庭安全入住。孩子们在昔日的会客室里摆满书籍，厨房里也重新升起饭菜的热气。奥尔德老宅本是为一个富裕家庭修建的，但在它最重要的时刻，却成了许多人的家。
