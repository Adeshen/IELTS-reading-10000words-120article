# 34. The Night Shift at Harbour Mall（海港商场的夜班）

**主题：** 建筑场所　 **目标词：** 40

When a cyclone approached Port Ellis, the council chose its largest **mall** as an emergency shelter. The **spacious** building contained a department **store**, a **supermarket**, a public **bath**, and even a closed **casino**. It stood on the **peripheral** edge of town, near a **viaduct** whose longest **span** crossed the river. Because no other shelter existed in the **vicinity**, engineer Sofia Reyes had one night to make the building safe for two thousand people.

Her inspection exposed trouble everywhere. A **void** beneath the entrance **plaza** had weakened its **tile** surface, and rain had damaged the surrounding **brickwork**. A veteran **mason** began filling the gap while Sofia examined the mall's **layout**. The **exterior** looked solid, but an **external** drain drove water **inward** through the wall. In the **interior**, the **inner** fire doors no longer formed an **airtight** barrier. Beyond the windows stretched an **expanse** of black water, already rising toward the road.

Workers erected a **scaffold** so they could **mend** the drain and **modify** its angle. Sofia told one team to **plumb** a new pipe and **install** a pump; correct **placement** was essential. Another team used each **screw**, **lever**, and **hook** carefully while they tried to **fix** the doors. Above them, a **crane** lifted a steel brace with its **suspension** cable. Then the machine suffered a **malfunction**. The brace swung into the wall, damaging the emergency power **apparatus** and threatening the town's water **infrastructure** controls inside the mall.

Sofia stopped the crane and changed the plan. The crew lowered the brace by hand, then **welded** it across two supports. Electricians repaired the apparatus, and plumbers diverted the leak. They worked until dawn not only to restore the building but also to **maintain** its pumps throughout the storm. Minutes before the first families arrived, the final door sealed properly. The cyclone flooded the plaza and closed the viaduct, but the shelter remained dry. What had seemed like an ordinary shopping centre became the safest place in Port Ellis.

## 中文翻译

飓风逼近埃利斯港时，市政委员会选择当地最大的**购物中心**作为应急避难所。这座**宽敞的**建筑内有一家百货**商店**、一家**超级市场**、一处公共**浴室**，甚至还有一家已经停业的**赌场**。它位于城镇**外围的**边缘，靠近一座横跨河流的**高架桥**，桥梁最长的**跨度**就在河面上方。由于**附近地区**没有其他避难场所，工程师索菲娅·雷耶斯只有一夜时间让这栋建筑足以安全容纳两千人。

检查很快暴露出各处的问题。入口**广场**下方的一个**空洞**削弱了地面的**瓷砖**，雨水也破坏了周围的**砖砌结构**。一位经验丰富的**泥瓦匠**开始填补缺口，索菲娅则检查商场的**布局**。建筑**外部**看似坚固，但一条**外接的**排水管却把水**向内**引进了墙体。在**内部空间**里，**内侧的**防火门也已无法形成**密封的**屏障。窗外是**一片广阔的**黑色水域，已经开始逼近道路。

工人们搭起**脚手架**，以便**修补**排水管并**调整**其角度。索菲娅让一组人**铺设管道**并**安装**新水泵；设备的正确**安放位置**至关重要。另一组人谨慎使用每一枚**螺钉**、每一根**杠杆**和每一个**吊钩**，努力**修好**防火门。头顶上方，一台**起重机**正用**悬吊**钢索吊起钢制支撑架。突然，机器发生**故障**。支撑架撞上墙壁，不仅损坏了应急供电**设备**，还威胁到设在商场内的城镇供水**基础设施**控制装置。

索菲娅叫停起重机并改变方案。工人们用手放下支撑架，随后把它**焊接**在两个支点之间。电工修复设备，水管工则把漏水改道。他们一直工作到黎明，不仅恢复了建筑功能，还要在整场风暴中**维护**水泵运行。第一批家庭抵达前几分钟，最后一扇门终于严密闭合。飓风淹没了广场，也封闭了高架桥，但避难所始终保持干燥。这个原本普通的购物中心，最终成了埃利斯港最安全的地方。
