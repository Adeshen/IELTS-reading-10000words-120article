# 35. The Parade on Mercury Road（水星路上的游行）

**主题：** 建筑场所／交通旅行　 **目标词：** 40

Retired **airline** **captain** David Okoro had spent thirty years in **aviation**, yet one flight continued to **haunt** him. His closest friend had died when their **airplane** became a **wreck** in the desert. Now David led an annual **memorial** **parade** to a stone **pyramid** built for the lost **crew**. This year, a restored cockpit would travel on a **wagon**, while a folded **parachute** rested on a display **rack** beside it.

David planned the **route** with an old **atlas** and gave every driver an **itinerary**. The procession would leave the museum **garage**, pass one busy **intersection**, follow a narrow **lane**, and stop beside the monument. A **tram**, a delivery **van**, a wooden **cart**, and a flower-covered **pedicab** joined the line. A local **safari** company supplied the towing **vehicle**, an **auto** with enough **horsepower** for the heavy wagon. Before departure, David checked its **internal** brakes, every **wheel**, and each **tyre**. He also marked a safe **path** around roadworks where workers were preparing to **pave** a damaged street.

At noon, dense **traffic** delayed the parade. A fallen **signpost** forced David to **navigate** toward the **curb**, but then the towing vehicle's steering locked. The driver sounded the **honk** as the wagon rolled downhill toward the crowded intersection. If it struck the tram, the impact could **crush** both the cockpit and the people beside it. David climbed into the cab, released a jammed cable, and turned the wagon into an empty freight yard. A **freight** truck blocked his escape, so he guided the wheels through a gate barely wider than the vehicle. For several seconds, the cockpit seemed to **vanish** behind a cloud of dust.

When the dust cleared, the wagon had stopped safely against a sand bank. No one was injured, though the axle required repair. The safari company brought another vehicle, and the procession reached the memorial before sunset. David placed his friend's name beneath the pyramid and understood why the parade mattered: it did not erase the old disaster, but it carried memory forward without allowing grief to control the road ahead.

## 中文翻译

退休的**航空公司****机长**戴维·奥科罗在**航空业**工作了三十年，但其中一次飞行仍然不断**困扰**着他。他最亲密的朋友在他们的**飞机**于沙漠中化为**残骸**时遇难。如今，戴维每年都会带领一场**纪念****游行**，前往一座为遇难**机组人员**修建的石制**金字塔**。今年，经过修复的驾驶舱将由一辆**四轮货车**运送，旁边的展示**架**上还放着一具折叠好的**降落伞**。

戴维利用一本旧**地图册**规划**路线**，并给每位驾驶员发了一份**行程安排**。队伍将从博物馆**车库**出发，经过一个繁忙的**十字路口**，沿一条狭窄的**车道**前行，最后停在纪念碑旁。一辆**有轨电车**、一辆送货**厢式货车**、一辆木制**手推车**和一辆饰满鲜花的**三轮车**也加入队伍。当地一家**野外观光**公司提供了牵引**车辆**——一辆拥有足够**马力**拖动沉重货车的**汽车**。出发前，戴维检查了车辆的**内部**制动装置、每个**车轮**以及每条**轮胎**。他还在道路施工区域旁标出一条安全**通道**；工人们正在那里准备**铺设**受损路面。

中午，拥挤的**交通**耽误了游行。一块倒下的**路标**迫使戴维把队伍**引导**到**路缘**旁，但牵引车的转向装置突然卡死。货车沿坡道滑向人群密集的十字路口时，司机不断按响**喇叭**。如果它撞上有轨电车，冲击力可能会**压碎**驾驶舱，并伤及旁边的人。戴维爬进驾驶室，松开一根卡住的钢索，将货车转入一处空置的货运场。一辆**货运**卡车挡住了出口，于是他让车轮穿过一道仅比车身稍宽的大门。有几秒钟，驾驶舱仿佛在尘土中**消失**了。

尘埃散去后，人们发现货车已经安全停在沙堆旁。没有人受伤，只是车轴需要修理。野外观光公司派来另一辆车，游行队伍终于在日落前抵达纪念碑。戴维把朋友的名字放在金字塔下，也明白了这场游行的意义：它无法抹去昔日的灾难，却能把记忆继续传递下去，不让悲伤控制前方的道路。
