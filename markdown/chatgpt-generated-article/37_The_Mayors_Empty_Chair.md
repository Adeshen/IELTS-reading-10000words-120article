# 37. The Mayor's Empty Chair（市长的空椅子）

**主题：** 国家政府　 **目标词：** 40

When Leila became **mayor** of Greyport, the old political **regime** had just fallen. The national **government** promised **democracy**, yet the town hall still obeyed a rigid **hierarchy** and a suffocating **bureaucracy**. Department heads guarded their power, while families in the riverside **slum** lacked clean water and basic public **service**. Leila, an **egalitarian** teacher who distrusted both privilege and crude **materialism**, hung neither a party **flag** nor a victory **banner** in her office. Instead, she placed an empty chair beside her desk as a reminder of citizens who had no voice.

Her first test came when the waste plant suffered a reactor **meltdown**. A **foreign** **consortium** offered money to rebuild it, but demanded the right to **dominate** the harbour for thirty years. Its lobbyists tried to **influence** every **municipal** **bureau**. The national **premier** remained publicly **neutral**, while his **secretary** quietly urged Leila to accept. In the capital, **parliament** and the **senate** arranged a joint **conference**. At the opening **meeting**, Leila faced an **overseas** **delegation** whose leader spoke like a great **statesman** but refused to reveal the contract's risks.

Leila proposed a **bilateral** review by local scientists and the investor's engineers. She turned the sports **arena** into a public hearing hall and invited residents to question both sides. The debate uncovered plans to move toxic waste to a former island **colony**, where migrant workers had little legal protection. Many had wanted to **emigrate** in search of **liberty** and **refuge** from poverty. Greyport's citizens rejected a prosperity built on their suffering.

The consortium withdrew, and the premier expected Leila's political **reign** to end. Newspapers called her a powerless **puppet** who had abandoned a golden **crown**. Yet local engineers designed a safer plant, funded by transparent taxes. On its opening day, children placed a green **wreath** on the empty chair. Leila joked that she owned no **throne** anyway. Her refusal had cost the town a quick solution, but it restored trust: government, people learned, could be firm without becoming secretive or cruel.

## 中文翻译

莱拉成为格雷波特的**市长**时，旧的政治**政权**刚刚倒台。国家**政府**承诺实行**民主**，然而市政厅仍受僵化的**等级制度**和令人窒息的**官僚体制**支配。各部门负责人牢牢守住自己的权力，河边**贫民窟**的家庭却没有洁净饮水和基本公共**服务**。莱拉原本是一名信奉**平等主义**的教师，既不信任特权，也不认同粗鄙的**物质主义**。她没有在办公室悬挂政党**旗帜**或胜选**横幅**，而是在办公桌旁放了一把空椅子，提醒自己还有许多公民无法发声。

她上任后的第一次考验来自垃圾处理厂的反应堆**熔毁事故**。一家**外国的****财团**提出出资重建，却要求获得港口三十年的控制权。其说客企图**支配**每一个**市政的****局**，并**影响**其中的官员。国家**总理**公开保持**中立**，他的**秘书**却私下催促莱拉接受条件。首都的**议会**和**参议院**安排了一场联合**大会**。在开幕**会议**上，莱拉面对一个**海外的****代表团**；团长说起话来像一位伟大的**政治家**，却拒绝公开合同中的风险。

莱拉提议由本地科学家和投资方工程师开展**双边的**审查。她把体育**竞技场**改成公共听证大厅，让居民向双方提问。辩论揭露了一个计划：把有毒废物运往一座昔日的海岛**殖民地**，而那里的外来工人几乎得不到法律保护。许多人本想**移居国外**，寻求**自由**，并逃离贫困、获得**庇护**。格雷波特市民拒绝接受建立在他们苦难之上的繁荣。

财团撤回投资，总理也以为莱拉的政治**执政时期**即将结束。报纸称她是一个无力的**傀儡**，丢掉了一顶金色**王冠**。然而，本地工程师设计出更安全的处理厂，并通过透明的税收筹集资金。开幕当天，孩子们在空椅子上放了一只绿色**花环**。莱拉开玩笑说，反正自己从来没有**王座**。她的拒绝使小镇失去了快捷方案，却重新赢得信任：人们终于明白，政府可以坚定，也可以不秘密、不残酷。
