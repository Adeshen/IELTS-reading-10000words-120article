# 40. The Copper Coin（那枚铜币）

**主题：** 社会经济　 **目标词：** 40

After her grandfather died, Nina inherited his **estate**, including a furniture business, and an unusual **legacy**: a worn **copper** **coin**, one old **shilling**, and an unprofitable warehouse. Her grandfather had begun with little **wealth**, buying **cheap** chairs at village markets and repairing anything he considered **worthy** of another life. Yet sales had entered a steep **decline**. Costs continued to **escalate**, customer numbers **diminished**, and the company lacked **sufficient** cash. Nina's accountant advised her to **downsize**, declare several workers **redundant**, and sell the property.

At the warehouse **auction**, an **affluent** investment **entity** made the highest **bid**. Its planned **acquisition** would turn the site into luxury flats. Nina would receive a large **deposit** immediately and the rest in one annual **instalment**. During the final **negotiation**, however, she noticed that Tomas, the master carpenter, was **absent**. He was at the port protecting their last **cargo**: imported timber that had not been properly **insured**. An unexpected customs **quota** had delayed the **import**, and the company was paying to store unsold **stock**.

Nina postponed the **transaction** and examined every business **account**. Online **sale** figures were rising even as shop **turnover** fell. An **influx** of young renters wanted durable desks, but the firm sold mainly to **wholesale** dealers. Tomas proposed they stop trying to **dump** old stock and invite customers to supply design **input**. Nina agreed to **invest** her inheritance in direct online orders. Workers would earn a small wage **increment** for learning digital tools, and buyers would receive a repair guarantee as an **incentive**.

The first year was difficult, but losses began to shrink. Nina rejected the property bid and converted half the warehouse into affordable workshops. Demand from affluent buyers helped finance discounted furniture for students. Looking at the copper coin above her desk, she realised why her grandfather had left it: value could **descend** through generations without being measured only in money. She had preserved neither every old product nor every old habit. Instead, she had preserved the principle beneath them—waste less, make useful things, and treat skilled people as the company's greatest asset.

## 中文翻译

祖父去世后，妮娜继承了他的家具**产业**，以及一份不同寻常的**遗产**：一枚磨损的**铜制的****硬币**、一枚旧**先令**和一座亏损的仓库。祖父创业时几乎没有什么**财富**，他在乡村市场购买**便宜的**椅子，并修复每件他认为**值得**重获新生的家具。然而，销售已经进入急剧**下降**期。成本持续**攀升**，顾客数量不断**减少**，公司也没有**足够的**现金。会计建议她**缩减规模**，宣布部分员工成为**多余的**岗位人员，并卖掉房产。

在仓库**拍卖会**上，一家**富裕的**投资**实体**提出了最高**出价**。它计划通过**收购**把这里改造成豪华公寓。妮娜可以立刻获得一大笔**定金**，余款则在一年后以一笔**分期付款**结清。然而，在最终**谈判**期间，她发现木工大师托马斯**缺席**了。他正在港口保护他们最后一批**货物**：一批没有得到妥善**投保**的进口木材。突如其来的海关**配额**延误了这批**进口商品**，公司还得付费储存未售出的**库存**。

妮娜推迟了这笔**交易**，并检查了每一本企业**账目**。网上**销售**数据正在上升，实体店的**营业额**却在下降。大量年轻租客的**涌入**带来了对耐用书桌的需求，但公司过去主要向**批发的**经销商供货。托马斯建议他们不要再试图**倾销**旧库存，而应邀请顾客提供设计**意见**。妮娜同意把遗产**投资**到直接网络订单业务中。工人学习数字工具后可以获得小幅工资**增量**，买家则能获得维修保证作为**激励**。

第一年十分艰难，但亏损开始缩小。妮娜拒绝了房产收购出价，并把仓库的一半改造成价格合理的工作室。来自富裕买家的需求帮助公司为学生提供折扣家具。看着办公桌上方那枚铜币，她终于明白祖父为何留下它：价值可以在几代人之间**传承下来**，而不必只用金钱衡量。她没有保留每一种旧产品，也没有沿袭每一种旧习惯；她保留的是背后的原则——减少浪费、制造有用的物品，并把有技能的人视为公司最重要的资产。
