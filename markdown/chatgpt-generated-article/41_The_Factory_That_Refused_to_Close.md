# 41. The Factory That Refused to Close（拒绝关门的工厂）

**主题：** 社会经济 / 法律法规　 **目标词：** 40

When a falling **salary** budget threatened the town's electric-bus factory, an overdue **payment** from a foreign buyer gave the owner a convenient excuse to **fire** half the **workforce**. He ordered Mira, the **payroll** manager, to prepare a mass **lay-off**. Each employee would receive one week's **wage**, no **remuneration** for unused leave, and no **refund** for tools they had bought. Mira's professional **career** had begun there; accounting was her **profession**, but protecting honest work felt like a **vocation**. Even this devoted **workaholic** refused to **dismiss**, **discharge**, or **sack** people by spreadsheet. Her **résumé** described a decade of skilled **labour** at the factory.

Mira found a green-industry **subsidy** that could **reimburse** training costs if the factory adopted a **sustainable** plan. She used the discovery to **prompt** an emergency meeting. The union and management signed an **agreement** to **cooperate**: engineers would **collaborate** with drivers, while Mira would **coordinate** spending. Their **charter** set one **rule**—no worker could **default** on safety duties—and a new **protocol** would **regulate** battery disposal. Government inspectors would **enforce** the environmental **standard**, using emissions from the best plant as a **benchmark** and imposing a **sanction** for violations.

One director tried to block the plan, arguing that the law should **allow** immediate dismissal. Mira produced the national labour **treaty**, whose equality **norm** protected permanent staff. She also challenged a rumour that health insurance covered **abortion** but not prenatal care; the actual policy covered both. Her calm evidence ended the argument. Six months later, improved buses won a major contract. The factory did not merely survive: employees helped **improve** it, and no one lost a job.

## 中文翻译

镇上的电动公交车厂因**薪资**预算不断缩水而面临危机，一名海外买家又拖欠了一笔**款项**，老板便以此为借口，打算**解雇**一半**员工队伍**。他命令负责**工资单**的米拉筹备大规模**裁员**：每位员工只能领到一周的**工资**，未休假期没有任何**报酬**，自行购买工具的钱也不予**退还**。米拉的职业**生涯**从这里起步；会计是她的**职业**，但维护诚实劳动更像她的**使命**。即使她一向是个投入工作的**工作狂**，也不愿仅凭一张表格就把人**解职**、**辞退**或**开除**。 她的**简历**记录了在工厂从事十年熟练**劳动**的经历。

米拉查到一项绿色产业**补贴**：只要工厂采用**可持续的**方案，政府便能**报销**培训费用。她以此**促使**管理层召开紧急会议。工会与管理层签署了一份**协议**，同意共同**合作**：工程师与司机开展**协作**，米拉则负责**协调**开支。他们的**章程**规定了一条**规则**——任何员工都不得在安全职责上**失责**——并制定新**规程**来**监管**电池处理。政府检查员将**执行**环保**标准**，以最佳工厂的排放水平作为**基准**，违规者会受到**制裁**。

一名董事试图阻挠计划，声称法律应当**允许**立即解雇。米拉拿出国家签署的劳动**条约**，其中的平等**规范**保护长期雇员。她还澄清了一则谣言：有人说医疗保险只涵盖**人工流产**，却不涵盖产前护理，实际政策两者都保障。她以冷静的证据终止了争论。六个月后，改良后的公交车赢得一份大合同。工厂不但保住了，员工还共同**改善**了它，没有一个人失业。
