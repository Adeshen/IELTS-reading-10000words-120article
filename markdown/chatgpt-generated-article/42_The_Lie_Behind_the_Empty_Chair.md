# 42. The Lie Behind the Empty Chair（空椅背后的谎言）

**主题：** 法律法规　 **目标词：** 40

At the first court **session**, an empty chair represented Tomas, a **mute** cleaner accused of helping a **thief** **rob** a charity. The **prosecutor** claimed he had **committed** online **fraud**, helped **steal** donations, and tried to **deceive** auditors. A strict bail **constraint** would **confine** him to **jail** if he missed any hearing. Yet his lawyer, Aisha, noticed clear **discrimination**: police had treated his inability to speak as guilt. They had ignored his written **plea** and called him a **liar**.

Aisha asked the **judge** to let an interpreter **mediate**. Tomas then explained that a manager had offered him a **bribe** to remain **silent** about false accounts. He had refused, and the manager threatened **suicide** before fleeing. Jurors heard guards **mutter** and spectators **murmur** as bank records revealed the truth. The charity's **supreme** governing board had failed to **oversee** or **supervise** its director. Its supposedly **mandatory** audit carried no **potent** safeguard, and vague rules merely **restrict** staff while doing little to **restrain** executives.

The evidence created a public **controversy**. Donors planned to **boycott** the charity and **sue** its board. Aisha argued that punishment must be based on **equity**, not fear; no personal **sin** could **justify** a false conviction. The **jury** cleared Tomas. Parliament later agreed to **enact** a whistle-blower law and **legislate** independent audits, placing a legal **obligation** on charities to report abuse. The real criminal was **prosecuted**; rumours about a digital **pirate** proved irrelevant. Tomas left court free, and the empty chair finally had an honest owner.

## 中文翻译

第一次庭审**开庭**时，一把空椅子代表托马斯——一名**失语的**清洁工。他被控协助一名**小偷****抢劫**慈善机构。**检察官**声称他**实施**了网络**诈骗**，帮忙**盗取**捐款，并企图**欺骗**审计人员。严格的保释**限制**规定，他若缺席任何庭审，就会被**关进****监狱**。然而，他的律师艾莎发现了明显的**歧视**：警方把他无法说话当成有罪的证据，既无视他的书面**申辩**，又称他为**骗子**。

艾莎请求**法官**允许手语翻译从中**调解沟通**。托马斯随后说明，一名经理曾拿**贿赂**让他对假账保持**沉默**。他拒绝后，经理扬言**自杀**，随后逃走。银行记录揭示真相时，陪审员听见法警在**低声抱怨**，旁听者则在**窃窃私语**。慈善机构的**最高**管理委员会没有**督察**或**监督**负责人。所谓**强制性的**审计缺乏**有力的**保障；模糊规定只会**限制**普通员工，却几乎不能**约束**高管。

这些证据引发公众**争议**。捐款人准备**抵制**该机构并**起诉**董事会。艾莎指出，惩罚必须建立在**公平**而非恐惧之上；任何个人的**罪过**都不能**证明**错误定罪合理。**陪审团**判托马斯无罪。议会后来同意**颁布**举报人保护法，并通过**立法**建立独立审计制度，使慈善机构承担报告滥权行为的法律**义务**。真正的罪犯受到**起诉**；有关网络**盗版者**的传闻则被证实与案件无关。托马斯自由地走出法院，那把空椅子终于等回了清白的主人。
