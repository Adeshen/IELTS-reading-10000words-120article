# 43. The Night the Museum Fought Back（博物馆反击之夜）

**主题：** 法律法规 / 沙场争锋　 **目标词：** 40

Curator Leila received an **anonymous** message: thieves would **raid** the hilltop war museum at midnight. Their target was not gold but an early rocket **blueprint** whose **copyright** belonged to a refugee engineer. The museum could **accredit** its **ownership**, yet online extremists claimed the document should be public. Their **petition** demanded unrestricted **access** and threatened an **explosion** if the museum did not **conform**. The emergency rules would **require** every visitor to show identification.

Leila studied every **clue**. A photograph showed a **twinkle** beside an old **cannon**; another revealed a fresh **glow** behind a display **shield**. She found wires hidden under **gunpowder** barrels and a tiny **spark** device disguised as a **flare**. The gang had also planted a replica **bomb**, a stolen **pistol**, a **rifle**, and one live **bullet** to create panic. Police could not risk a **blast** or **bombard** the building, whose glass cases would **deflect** fragments into visitors. They quietly **equipped** and **deployed** a specialist team instead.

When the raiders entered carrying a **blade**, an **ax**, and a crossbow **arrow**, Leila locked the archive remotely. No **missile** was launched and no charge detonated. Confronted by officers, the leader began to **confess**: the threat was meant to hide the **enormity** of a planned sale. He would face a heavy **penalty** and **fine**, while his house **mortgage** might be seized. Leila chose to **reconcile** with the young guard who had leaked a code under pressure rather than let him **succumb** to lifelong **stigma**. At dawn, sunlight made the medals **glitter**—not as trophies of violence, but as reminders that courage can protect memory without destroying it.

## 中文翻译

馆长莱拉收到一条**匿名的**消息：盗贼将在午夜**突袭**山顶战争博物馆。他们的目标不是黄金，而是一份早期火箭**蓝图**，其**版权**属于一名难民工程师。博物馆能够**认证**这份文献的**所有权**，但网络极端分子声称它应当公开。他们发起**请愿**，要求不受限制的**访问权**，还威胁说，若博物馆不肯**顺从**，就制造**爆炸**。 紧急规定将**要求**每位访客出示身份证明。

莱拉研究了每一条**线索**。一张照片显示旧**大炮**旁有一点**闪光**；另一张则显示陈列**盾牌**后出现新鲜的**亮光**。她在**火药**桶下发现电线，还找到一个伪装成信号**照明弹**的小型**火花**装置。团伙还放置了仿制**炸弹**、偷来的**手枪**、一支**步枪**和一枚真**子弹**，企图制造恐慌。警方既不能冒险引发**爆炸冲击**，也不能**炮击**建筑，因为玻璃展柜会使碎片**偏转**，伤及参观者。因此，他们悄悄**装备**并**部署**了一支专业队伍。

袭击者带着**刀刃**、**斧头**和弩**箭**闯入时，莱拉远程锁住了档案室。没有**导弹**发射，也没有炸药引爆。面对警察，首领开始**坦白**：所谓威胁只是为掩盖这桩非法交易的**严重程度**。他将面临严厉**处罚**和**罚款**，住房**抵押贷款**所对应的房产也可能被没收。那名年轻警卫是在威逼下泄露密码的，莱拉决定与他**和解**，不让他**屈服于**伴随终生的**污名**。黎明时，阳光让勋章熠熠**闪耀**——它们不再是暴力的战利品，而是在提醒人们：勇气也可以在不毁灭任何东西的情况下守护记忆。
