# 44. The Bell Beneath the Occupation（占领下的钟声）

**主题：** 沙场争锋　 **目标词：** 40

During a **turbulent** winter, an army began a **siege** of Mara's mountain town. Soldiers **invaded** farms, **enclosed** the square, and threatened to **deprive** residents of grain during a spreading **famine**. Anyone who **trespassed** near the storehouse was beaten; officers would **intrude** into homes and **violate** family privacy. The people's **plight** grew **terrible**, but Mara, who held the mayor's horse's **rein**, noticed a crucial **pitfall** in the occupiers' plan: they had left an old aqueduct unguarded.

Some citizens demanded immediate **revolt**. A furious **rebel** wanted to **punch** a guard, **stab** the commander, and **chop** the bridge supports. Mara warned that such an **assault** would **offend** neutral villages and bring a **terrific** retaliation. Others suspected a **conspiracy** and called her a **traitor**. She answered their **reproach** with **proof**: maps showed the aqueduct could carry food, not fighters. A true **patriot**, she argued, should **deter** cruelty without copying it. The occupiers called every act of resistance **treason**.

At night, volunteers used the passage to **pursue** a safer **scenario**. They supplied the **destitute**, then rang the hidden town bell. Its sound told neighbouring communities to block the army's supply road. Growing **tension** turned into **turmoil** among the occupiers. Their commander ordered troops to **halt** the raids and **cease** the occupation before hunger reached them too. He could no longer **oppress** the town or **prohibit** relief without losing his own men. Mara had felt **dread**, but no one had needed to **hit** or kill. The town defeated a **threat** through restraint, solidarity, and one forgotten waterway.

## 中文翻译

在一个局势**动荡的**冬天，一支军队开始**围困**玛拉所在的山城。士兵**入侵**农田，**封闭**广场，并威胁在蔓延的**饥荒**中**剥夺**居民的粮食。任何**擅闯**粮仓附近的人都会挨打；军官还会**闯入**民宅，**侵犯**家庭隐私。百姓的**困境**变得十分**可怕**，但替市长牵着马匹**缰绳**的玛拉发现了占领计划中的关键**隐患**：一条旧引水渠无人看守。

一些市民要求立刻**起义**。一名愤怒的**反抗者**想**猛击**卫兵，**刺伤**指挥官，再**砍断**桥梁支柱。玛拉警告说，这样的**袭击**会**冒犯**中立村庄，并招致**极其猛烈的**报复。另一些人怀疑存在**阴谋**，骂她是**叛徒**。她用**证据**回应众人的**责备**：地图表明，引水渠可以运送食物，而不是输送战士。她说，真正的**爱国者**应当**阻止**暴行，而不是仿效暴行。 占领者把每一次抵抗都称为**叛国罪**。

夜里，志愿者利用通道**争取实现**一个更安全的**方案**。他们救济了**一贫如洗的人**，随后敲响隐藏的镇钟。钟声通知邻近村庄封锁军队的补给道路。不断上升的**紧张局势**变成占领军内部的**混乱**。指挥官命令部队**停止**抢掠，并在自己的士兵也陷入饥饿前**终止**占领。他再也无法继续**压迫**小镇，也无法**禁止**救援而不失去部下。玛拉虽然心怀**恐惧**，却没有人需要**击打**或杀害谁。这座城依靠克制、团结和一条被遗忘的水道化解了**威胁**。
