# 46. The Veteran's Final Patrol（老兵的最后一次巡山）

**主题：** 沙场争锋 / 社会角色　 **目标词：** 40

At eighty, Arun was a **veteran**, a former **soldier**, and the last living **scout** from the valley's independence campaign. Every spring he led a **patrol** to a hilltop **monument**, but this year he vanished. His **grandfather** had been a mountain **pioneer**, and Arun believed an old **prophet** had named their family the monument's safeguard. Newspapers called him a national **hero** and **herald** of peace, yet age had made him too proud to admit weakness. Their mission was to **safeguard** the mountain village.

His extended family began searching: Arun's **heir**, his adopted son; the son's **spouse**; a married **couple** of cousins; Arun's **sibling**; and his worried **father-in-law**. Their different **gender** and ages mattered less than their shared **surname**. Following torn **camouflage**, they found a narrow **burrow** beside the trail. It was not an animal's home but a wartime tunnel. A fallen beam formed an **obstacle**, and every shout seemed to **shrink** in the darkness. One survivor was reunited with her **husband** before nightfall.

Inside, Arun had refused to **retreat**, **withdraw**, or **surrender** when the roof cracked. He had tried to **withstand** the pain and **forgo** rescue so others would not **interfere** with a nearby wildlife survey. Worse, he had been **misled** by an old map. The family **discarded** pride, dug through the beam, and managed to **capture** his faint reply on a radio. They also found an abandoned **infant**, an **orphan** left by fleeing smugglers, beside a preserved animal **embryo** stolen from a laboratory.

Saving both lives became the true **victory** and **triumph**; there was no enemy to **conquer**. Arun named his daughter as **successor** to the memorial trust and asked it to support every vulnerable **juvenile** in the valley. At the next ceremony, he said the most **glorious** courage was knowing when to accept help.

## 中文翻译

八十岁的阿伦是一名**退伍军人**，也是一位昔日的**士兵**，更是山谷独立运动中最后健在的**侦察兵**。每年春天，他都会带队**巡查**山顶**纪念碑**，但今年他失踪了。他的**祖父**曾是山区的**开拓者**，阿伦还相信一位老**先知**曾说，他们家族注定要守护纪念碑。报纸称他为民族**英雄**和和平的**先驱**，年龄却让他骄傲得不肯承认衰弱。 他们的任务是**保护**山村。

大家族开始搜寻：阿伦的**继承人**——他的养子，养子的**配偶**，一对已婚表亲**夫妇**，阿伦的**兄弟姐妹**，以及焦急的**岳父**。众人的**性别**和年龄并不重要，共同的**姓氏**把他们连在一起。他们沿着撕破的**伪装布**前进，在小路旁发现一个狭窄**洞穴**。那里不是兽穴，而是一条战时地道。坠落的横梁成为**障碍**，所有呼喊似乎都在黑暗中**减弱**。 一名幸存者在天黑前与她的**丈夫**团聚。

地道里，屋顶开裂时，阿伦既不肯**撤退**、**撤离**，也不肯**投降**。他试图**忍受**疼痛，还想**放弃**获救机会，以免别人**干扰**附近的野生动物调查。更糟的是，一张旧地图**误导**了他。家人**抛开**自尊，挖开横梁，并通过无线电**捕捉到**他微弱的回应。他们还发现一个被走私者逃跑时遗弃的**婴儿**——一个**孤儿**，旁边则有从实验室偷出的动物**胚胎**标本。

救下两条生命才是真正的**胜利**与**凯旋**，这里没有需要**征服**的敌人。阿伦指定女儿为纪念基金的**继任者**，并要求基金帮助山谷中每一名弱势**未成年人**。在下一次纪念仪式上，他说，最**光荣的**勇气，是懂得何时接受帮助。
