# 47. The Wedding at Number Seventeen（十七号公寓的婚礼）

**主题：** 社会角色　 **目标词：** 40

Nora, a daily **commuter**, rented one room in a crowded **household** at Number Seventeen. The **landlady**, Mrs Bell, was a cheerful **widow**; the absent **landlord** was her brother. Other residents included a shy **youngster**, an elderly **tenant**, and a visiting **client** of Nora's design firm. Each had a **respective** **chore**, and Nora's was cooking for every **guest** Mrs Bell agreed to **host**.

One evening Nora had an unexpected **encounter** with Elias, her former **supervisor**. He was no longer her **manager**, and she was no longer his **subordinate**. During their **adolescence** they had belonged to the same cycling **gang**, but work had forced them to **separate**. Elias had remained **single**; Nora had no **dependent** and lived a **sole**, independent life. Their old **friendship** returned, then became an **intimate** bond. Mrs Bell called them “my **dear** children,” while the youngster teasingly said “**darling**” whenever either entered the kitchen.

Elias planned to **propose**, but a **stranger** arrived claiming to be his secret **lover**. She was actually his **beloved** cousin and closest **kin**, delivering his grandmother's ring. “**Madam**,” she told Mrs Bell, “I am neither his **mistress** nor a jealous **maiden**.” Everyone laughed, though Nora admitted she had begun to **miss** him before the truth emerged.

The couple announced their **engagement**, decided to **marry**, and skipped an expensive **honeymoon**. Instead, each **individual** resident transformed the courtyard into a wedding hall. Mrs Bell's **husband** had planted its roses long ago, so she laid his photograph beside the cake. Elias greeted every old **comrade**, and Nora realised that a house full of people once unknown to one another had become a family.

## 中文翻译

诺拉每天都是一名**通勤者**，租住在十七号公寓这个拥挤的**家庭住所**里。**女房东**贝尔太太是一位开朗的**寡妇**；不常露面的**房东**则是她哥哥。其他住客包括一个腼腆的**年轻人**、一位年长**租客**，以及到访的诺拉设计公司的**客户**。每个人都有**各自的****家务**，诺拉负责为贝尔太太接待的每一位**客人**做饭，而贝尔太太则充当**主人**。

一天晚上，诺拉意外**遇见**前任**主管**埃利亚斯。他已不再是她的**经理**，她也不再是他的**下属**。两人在**青春期**曾属于同一个骑行**伙伴群**，后来工作迫使他们**分开**。埃利亚斯一直**单身**；诺拉也没有需要抚养的**家属**，过着**独自的**独立生活。旧日**友谊**重新萌发，渐渐成为**亲密的**感情。贝尔太太称他们是“我**亲爱的**孩子”，年轻住客则每逢两人进厨房就故意喊一声“**宝贝**”。

埃利亚斯打算**求婚**，一名**陌生人**却突然出现，自称是他的秘密**情人**。其实她是他最**心爱的**表妹，也是关系最近的**亲属**，专程送来祖母的戒指。“**女士**，”她对贝尔太太说，“我既不是他的**情妇**，也不是吃醋的**未婚女子**。”众人都笑了；诺拉则承认，真相揭晓前，她已经开始**想念**埃利亚斯。

这对情侣宣布**订婚**，决定**结婚**，并放弃昂贵的**蜜月**。公寓里的每个**个人**都动手把院子改造成婚礼大厅。贝尔太太已故的**丈夫**多年前种下了院里的玫瑰，于是她把他的照片放在蛋糕旁。埃利亚斯迎接每一位旧**伙伴**，诺拉终于明白：这座原本住着陌生人的房子，已经变成了一个家。
