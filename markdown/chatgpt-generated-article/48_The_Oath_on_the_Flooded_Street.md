# 48. The Oath on the Flooded Street（洪水街头的誓言）

**主题：** 社会角色 / 行为动作　 **目标词：** 40

When the river burst its banks, an armed thief took the town **cashier** **hostage** inside the market. Lina, an **apprentice** **veterinary** nurse, saw a frightened horse collapse beside them. The local **council** was meeting nearby: its **chancellor**, a regional **delegate**, a legal **consultant**, a **solicitor**, and an **attorney** had gathered to debate flood barriers. Lina's mother, a **counsellor**, urged everyone to **behave** calmly rather than **react** in fear.

Help came from unlikely places. A **fireman** became rescue **commander** and sent his **deputy** to clear the street. A **gardener** and **fisherman** rowed families to safety; the **butcher** cut ropes; a wedding **groom** carried children; and a former football **umpire** directed traffic. Even a hungry **beggar** helped **mow** reeds blocking a drain. No one wanted to be a **coward**, though hours of **toil** left them exhausted.

Lina approached the horse, took one careful **intake** of breath, then slowly **exhaled**. Through a broken shutter she caught a **glimpse** of the hostage and dared to **peep** again. She **gazed** at the thief, then **peered** at his shaking hand. “Before you act, **contemplate** what happens next,” she said. She offered to **nourish** and **revive** his injured horse if he released the cashier.

The thief surrendered. The chancellor called Lina a **heroine**, but she named the whole town the **hero**. As a **proponent** of better emergency planning, she asked the council to make a public **pledge**. Its members took an **oath** and **vowed** to build the barriers before the next rainy season. Their promise began not in a grand chamber, but on a flooded street.

## 中文翻译

河水冲破堤岸时，一名持械盗贼在市场里把镇上的**收银员**劫为**人质**。**兽医的**护理**学徒**莉娜看见一匹受惊的马倒在两人身旁。地方**议会**正在附近开会；**议长**、地区**代表**、法律**顾问**、一名**律师**和一名**代理律师**原本正讨论防洪屏障。莉娜的母亲是一位心理**咨询师**，她劝大家冷静**行动**，不要因恐惧而贸然**反应**。

各行各业的人赶来帮忙。一个**消防员**担任救援**指挥官**，派自己的**副手**清理街道。**园丁**和**渔民**划船转移居民；**屠夫**割断绳索；婚礼上的**新郎**抱走孩子；前足球**裁判员**指挥交通。就连饥饿的**乞丐**也帮忙**割除**堵住排水口的芦苇。没有人愿意当**懦夫**，尽管数小时的**辛苦劳动**已使大家筋疲力尽。

莉娜走近那匹马，小心地**吸入**一口气，再缓缓**呼出**。透过破损的百叶窗，她**瞥见**了人质，又冒险**偷看**一次。她先**凝视**盗贼，随后仔细**端详**他颤抖的手。“行动前，请先**深思**接下来会发生什么。”她提出，只要对方释放收银员，她就会**喂养**并**救醒**受伤的马。

盗贼投降了。议长称莉娜是**女英雄**，她却说全镇人民才是**英雄**。作为改善应急规划的**支持者**，她请求议会公开作出**保证**。议员们立下**誓言**并**发誓**，要在下一个雨季前建好屏障。他们的承诺并非始于宏伟的会议厅，而是始于一条被洪水淹没的街道。
