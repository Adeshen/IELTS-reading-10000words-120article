# 49. The Tram on Copper Bridge（铜桥上的电车）

**主题：** 行为动作　 **目标词：** 40

Control called to **notify** engineer Mei that a driverless tram was racing toward Copper Bridge. She did not **panic** or **hurry** blindly, but told her crew to **hasten** to the maintenance car. Managers began to **scold**, **curse**, and **swear** over the radio. Mei calmly **asserted** that blame would only **provoke** mistakes. She would **explain** the failure later; now there was no time to **dispute** or even **mention** old grievances.

Their car accelerated to **overtake** the tram and establish radio **contact**. A cable could **tow** it, but first a technician had to **grab** the coupling and keep a firm **grip**. Loose tools began to **vibrate**, **shuffle**, and **scatter** as both vehicles **swayed**. Wheels **rotated** so fast that sparks seemed to **whirl**. If the cars **collided**, steel could **split**, glass could **smash**, and sharp edges might **scrape** or **pierce** passengers.

Mei ordered the crew to **loosen** one brake, **twist** a valve, and **tilt** the towing arm. A mechanic reached out to **snatch** the cable before it could **penetrate** a window. The coupling made a violent **touch**, but held. Mei then **integrated** the tram's emergency system with her controls and slowed both vehicles before the bridge.

On the platform, relieved passengers began to **clap**. One elderly man tried to **kneel** in thanks, but Mei stopped him. She did not **boast**, **preach**, or **mock** the frightened driver who had made a programming **mess**. Instead, she documented how a loose sensor had caused the fault. Her discipline turned a chain of moving metal into a rescue rather than a collision.

## 中文翻译

控制中心来电**通知**工程师梅：一辆无人驾驶电车正冲向铜桥。她没有盲目**慌忙**，而是让团队**赶紧**登上检修车。管理人员开始通过无线电**训斥**、**咒骂**，甚至**发誓诅咒**。梅冷静地**断言**，相互指责只会**激起**更多错误。她稍后会**解释**故障；此刻没时间**争论**，甚至不该**提及**旧怨。

检修车加速**超过**电车，并与它建立无线电**联系**。钢索可以**拖曳**电车，但技术员必须先**抓住**连接器并牢牢**握紧**。两辆车不停**摇摆**，松动的工具随之**振动**、**挪动**并四处**散落**。车轮高速**旋转**，火花仿佛在空中**打旋**。一旦两车**相撞**，钢材可能**裂开**，玻璃可能**粉碎**，锐利边缘还可能**刮伤**或**刺穿**乘客。

梅命令团队**松开**一处制动器，**扭动**阀门，并使拖臂**倾斜**。一名机械师伸手**夺住**钢索，避免它**穿透**车窗。连接器猛烈**碰触**了一下，却稳稳扣住。随后，梅把电车的应急系统与自己的控制器**整合**起来，让两辆车在抵达桥梁前减速。

站台上，如释重负的乘客开始**鼓掌**。一位老人想**跪下**致谢，梅却扶住了他。她没有**吹嘘**、**说教**或**嘲笑**那名因编程而弄出一团**混乱**的司机，而是记录下松动传感器引发故障的全过程。她的沉着让一连串飞驰的金属最终成为救援，而非撞车事故。
