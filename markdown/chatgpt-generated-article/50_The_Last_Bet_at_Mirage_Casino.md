# 50. The Last Bet at Mirage Casino（幻影赌场的最后赌注）

**主题：** 行为动作　 **目标词：** 40

Detective Samira followed a paper **trail** to Mirage Casino, where a criminal network used bright shows to **mesmerise** tourists and **allure** desperate gamblers. Its owner would **tempt** guests with a free **bet**, then **induce** them to raise the **stake** and **gamble** away their savings. Cameras helped staff **manipulate** anyone who became **obsessed**. The **irony** was that the building had once been **baptised** “Hope House.”

Samira's mission would **involve** more than gambling. The gang planned to **kidnap** a witness, **hijack** a bus, and **smuggle** people across the border. A guard tried to **bully** her informant and **strangle** him with a wire. Samira chose to **intervene**. She used a delivery van to **steer** through a service gate, though barriers **impeded** her and mirrored corridors could **bewilder** anyone who **deviated** from the map.

In the basement she found historical weapons marked as trophies from a **massacre** and the **decimation** of a village. The display filled her with **contempt** and recalled the **indignity** suffered by survivors. It also exposed the owner's **bias**, **ignorance**, and deliberate **disregard** for truth. He had used fear as **bait**, while officials' **neglect** allowed the business to grow.

Police freed the witness before **panic** spread. A court would **convict** the owner and **expel** his foreign partners. Samira did not seek **revenge** or let the case become a **nightmare** that would **obsess** her. Authorities **designated** the building a survivor-support centre and **assigned** her informant a protection officer. Visitors would soon **marvel** that a place built to exploit weakness had become a shelter from it.

## 中文翻译

萨米拉警探沿着一条文件**踪迹**追查到幻影赌场。犯罪网络用绚丽表演**迷住**游客，并以诱惑**吸引**走投无路的赌徒。老板先用免费**赌注****引诱**客人，再**诱使**他们提高**押注金额**，把积蓄全部**赌掉**。摄像头帮助员工**操纵**那些对赌博产生**痴迷**的人。颇具**讽刺意味**的是，这栋建筑曾被**命名洗礼为**“希望之家”。

萨米拉的任务不只**涉及**赌博。团伙还计划**绑架**证人、**劫持**巴士，并将人口**走私**出境。一名保安试图**欺凌**线人，并用铁丝**勒死**他。萨米拉决定**介入**。她驾驶送货车穿过服务入口，努力**操控方向**；障碍物不断**阻碍**她，而镜面走廊足以让任何**偏离**地图的人感到**困惑**。

地下室里陈列着历史武器，被当作一场**大屠杀**和某村庄人口遭到**大批杀戮**的战利品。这场展示令她充满**蔑视**，也让人想起幸存者遭受的**屈辱**。它还暴露了老板的**偏见**、**无知**以及对真相的刻意**漠视**。他把恐惧当作**诱饵**，而官员的**疏忽**则让生意不断扩张。

警方在**恐慌**蔓延前救出证人。法院将**判定**老板有罪，并**驱逐**他的外国同伙。萨米拉没有寻求**报复**，也没有让案件变成令她终日**困扰**的**噩梦**。政府把大楼**指定**为幸存者援助中心，并为线人**指派**了一名保护官。游客很快会为此**惊叹**：一个曾经利用弱点牟利的地方，如今竟成了庇护人们的所在。
