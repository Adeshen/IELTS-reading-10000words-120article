# 51. The Crates Marked Not for Sale（标着“非卖品”的救援箱）

**主题：** 行为动作　 **目标词：** 40

After a cyclone, relief coordinator Hana prepared to **launch** a supply mission to Bell Island. At dawn, trucks would **dispatch** food to the harbour and boats would **distribute** it among villages. The contract **stipulated** that no official could **exclude** remote families or **exploit** the emergency. Hana wanted to **assure** islanders that aid would arrive and **reassure** frightened volunteers, but dark clouds continued to **loom** offshore. The rescue mission would officially **commence** at dawn.

At the warehouse, Hana discovered medicine crates in **disguise** beneath commercial labels. A corrupt clerk had tried to **conceal** them, **detach** their tracking tags, and **erase** the records. He planned to **dispose** of cheap supplies, **omit** two villages from the route, and **cancel** their delivery so he could sell the medicine privately. When confronted, he **refused** to speak. Hana used backup files to **reverse** his changes, **undo** the false orders, and **rectify** the inventory.

The storm forced the team to **resort** to fishing boats, yet they did not abandon their purpose. Volunteers **strove** to **fulfil** every promise. They had to **tackle** high waves, **cope** with seasickness, and **exert** all their strength to unload the cargo. Their **effort** began to **restore** trust. Food would **replenish** empty kitchens, generators would **supply** power, and canvas sheets would **render** damaged schools usable again.

The mission helped Hana **redeem** an earlier failure that still made her **yearn** for a second chance. She had always **aspired** to serve isolated communities; now the work seemed to **invoke** that old ambition and satisfy an **itch** to act. The team used larger boats to **enlarge** capacity and solar chargers to **augment** limited power, helping **offset** transport delays. By sunset, villagers had received every crate, and no one could turn disaster relief into private profit.

## 中文翻译

飓风过后，救援协调员哈娜准备**启动**前往贝尔岛的物资任务。黎明时，卡车会把食品**发送**到港口，再由船只向各村**分发**。合同明确**规定**，任何官员都不得**排除**偏远家庭，也不得**利用**灾情牟利。哈娜希望向岛民**保证**援助一定会抵达，也想**安慰**惶恐的志愿者，然而海面上仍有乌云**逼近**。 救援任务将于黎明正式**开始**。

在仓库里，哈娜发现药箱被商业标签**伪装**起来。一名腐败职员企图**隐藏**药品，**拆下**追踪标签并**抹去**记录。他打算**处理掉**廉价物资，从路线中**省略**两个村庄，再**取消**配送，以便私下出售药品。面对质问，他**拒绝**开口。哈娜利用备份文件**逆转**其改动，**撤销**虚假指令，并**纠正**库存记录。

暴风雨迫使团队**求助于**渔船，但他们没有放弃目标。志愿者奋力**争取**，要**履行**每一项承诺。他们必须**应对**巨浪、**克服**晕船，并**使出**全部力气卸货。这番**努力**开始**恢复**岛民的信任。食物将**补充**空荡的厨房，发电机会**供应**电力，帆布则能使受损学校重新**变得**可用。

这次任务也让哈娜有机会**弥补**一次旧日失败；那次失败一直使她**渴望**第二次机会。她始终**立志**服务与世隔绝的社区，如今这项工作仿佛**唤起**了旧日抱负，也满足了她迫切行动的那股**渴望**。团队用更大的船**扩大**运力，并用太阳能充电器**增加**有限电力，从而**抵消**运输延误。日落前，每个村庄都收到了全部救援箱，再没有人能把救灾援助变成私人利润。
