# 52. The Siren Beneath the Applause（掌声下的警报）

**主题：** 行为动作　 **目标词：** 40

During a chemical plant's anniversary, director Vale used giant screens to **magnify** his achievements and loudspeakers to **amplify** every compliment. Guests came to **celebrate**, **congratulate** him, and **salute** retiring workers. Vale loved to **flatter** investors and expected employees to **worship** efficiency for profit's **sake**. Safety officer Priya, however, refused to **exaggerate** the factory's record. Her sole **objective** was to **uphold** the truth. The spectacle could **arouse** excitement throughout the town.

Behind the stage, a cracked pipe began to **leak**. Solvent would **seep** through a wall, **ooze** beneath a door, and finally **spill** into a drainage **trench**. Sensors designed to **trigger** an alarm failed because Vale had disconnected their **backup** to avoid interrupting the ceremony. Priya could **specify** the broken valve and **verify** the rising pressure, but music continued to **muffle** her warnings. Smoke began to **overshadow** the lights.

Priya seized a microphone. She would not **excuse** Vale's decision, yet begged the crowd to **regard** evacuation as a precaution, not panic. Workers helped **evacuate** guests before fumes could **saturate** the hall. Engineers used pumps to **propel** contaminated water into sealed tanks. Then the share price began to **plummet**, and Vale tried to **attribute** the accident to Priya. Retrieved messages allowed her to **testify** and **retrieve** the missing maintenance report; the dates **corresponded** exactly.

An inquiry chose not to **prolong** blame. It demanded reform and asked workers to **assort** damaged equipment for inspection. Priya helped **enlighten** managers and **impart** practical safety lessons. Vale apologised, and she agreed to **forgive** him only after he accepted responsibility. Months later, workers **welcomed** Priya as the new director. At Vale's quiet **farewell**, she found genuine **pleasure** in seeing safety valued more than applause.

## 中文翻译

化工厂举办周年庆典时，厂长维尔用巨型屏幕**放大**自己的成就，又让扩音器**扩大**每一句赞美。来宾前来**庆祝**、向他**祝贺**，并向退休工人**致敬**。维尔喜欢**奉承**投资人，还要求员工为了利润的**缘故****崇拜**效率。然而，安全主管普里娅拒绝**夸大**工厂的安全记录。她唯一的**目标**是**维护**真相。 这场盛会足以在全城**激起**兴奋。

舞台后方，一条开裂管道开始**泄漏**。溶剂先**渗过**墙壁，再从门下**缓缓流出**，最终**溢入**排水**沟槽**。原本用于**触发**警报的传感器失效了，因为维尔为避免打断庆典，切断了它们的**备用系统**。普里娅能够**明确指出**损坏的阀门，也能**核实**压力正在上升，但音乐仍不断**压低掩盖**她的警告，烟雾也开始**遮蔽**灯光。

普里娅夺过话筒。她不会为维尔的决定**开脱**，却请求人群把撤离**看作**预防措施，而不是恐慌。工人协助**疏散**来宾，避免烟气**充满**大厅。工程师用泵把受污染的水**推动**到密封罐中。随后公司股价开始**暴跌**，维尔却试图把事故**归因于**普里娅。找回的信息使她得以出庭**作证**，她还**取回**了失踪的维修报告，其中日期完全**相符**。

调查组没有选择**延长**相互指责，而是要求改革，并让工人**分类整理**受损设备以供检查。普里娅帮助管理人员**认识理解**问题，并向他们**传授**实用的安全经验。维尔道了歉；只有在他承担责任后，她才同意**原谅**他。几个月后，工人们**欢迎**普里娅担任新厂长。在维尔低调的**告别**仪式上，她感到真正的**愉悦**：安全终于比掌声更受重视。
