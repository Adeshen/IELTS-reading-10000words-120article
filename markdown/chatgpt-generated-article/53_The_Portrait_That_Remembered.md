# 53. The Portrait That Remembered（记得往事的肖像）

**主题：** 行为动作 / 身心健康　 **目标词：** 40

After a climbing accident, illustrator Tomas could not **recall** his daughter's face. He could **recollect** the fall and **retell** the rescue, yet when asked to **repeat** her name, shame would **mortify** him. In **retrospect**, doctors believed trauma had altered how his **brain** linked memory and **emotion**. Fear began to **pervade** his every **mood**, changing his gentle **temper** and lively **personality**.

Therapist Mei asked Tomas to draw a human figure. He began with its **stature**, then the **skeleton**, **backbone**, and **skull**. He added each **limb**, **shoulder**, **elbow**, **palm**, **thumb**, and **finger**. Slowly he drew the **neck**, **chin**, **jaw**, **mouth**, and **tongue**, then the **brow** and a single **eyelash**. He marked a **rib** and the **abdomen**, carefully trying to **differentiate** anatomy from memory. Whenever his paper began to **incline**, Mei told him not to **lean** so hard on the table.

One afternoon, Tomas drew a mountain **gorge** behind the figure. The feature startled him: his daughter had once painted that same place. A tiny scar above the figure's eye became the **trait** that unlocked everything. Memory returned—not as a dramatic vision, but as the sound of his daughter laughing with paint on her nose.

Tomas still had to **surmount** headaches, but he no longer viewed illness as weakness. Patience became his strongest **virtue**. At his next exhibition, the finished portrait did not merely **impress** viewers through technical skill; every bodily **feature** showed the path by which a father had found his child again.

## 中文翻译

一次登山事故后，插画家托马斯无法**回想起**女儿的面容。他能够**忆起**坠落经过，也能**复述**救援过程，可一旦被要求**重复**女儿的名字，羞耻感就会令他**窘迫**。**回顾往事**，医生认为创伤改变了他的**大脑**连接记忆与**情感**的方式。恐惧开始**弥漫**在每一种**心境**里，改变了他温和的**脾气**与活泼的**个性**。

治疗师梅请托马斯画一幅人体。他先勾勒人物的**身材高度**，再画出**骨骼**、**脊柱**和**头骨**。随后，他添上每一条**肢体**、**肩膀**、**肘部**、**手掌**、**拇指**和**手指**。他慢慢画出**颈部**、**下巴**、**下颌**、**嘴巴**和**舌头**，又画上**眉部**与一根**睫毛**；接着标出一根**肋骨**和**腹部**，努力把解剖知识与记忆**区分开**。每当画纸开始**倾斜**，梅都会提醒他不要太用力**倚靠**桌子。

一天下午，托马斯在人物背后画出一道山间**峡谷**。这个细节让他突然一惊：女儿以前画过同一个地方。人物眼睛上方的一道小疤痕，成了打开全部记忆的关键**特征**。记忆回来了——不是戏剧性的幻象，而是女儿鼻尖沾着颜料时的笑声。

托马斯仍需**克服**头痛，却不再把疾病视为软弱。耐心成了他最强大的**美德**。下一次画展中，完成的肖像不仅凭技巧给观众留下深刻**印象**；画中每一处身体**特点**，都展示了一位父亲重新找到女儿的道路。
