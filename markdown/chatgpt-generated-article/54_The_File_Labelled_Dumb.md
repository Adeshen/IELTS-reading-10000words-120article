# 54. The File Labelled “Dumb”（标着“哑巴”的病历）

**主题：** 身心健康　 **目标词：** 40

Medical student Amara opened an old village file that described twelve-year-old Ben as “deaf and **dumb**.” Ben was neither unable to think nor unable to speak; an untreated **aural** **infection** had damaged his hearing, and adults had mistaken his silence for ignorance. A **vague** note also called him **lame**, although a **visible** scar on his **heel** showed that tight shoes—not a crippled **muscle**—made him limp.

Amara offered a complete examination. Ben remained **awake**, though he had begun to **doze** during long lessons and often needed a **nap** after school. She checked every **joint**, from **shoulder** to **knee**, and tested each **nerve** from **thigh** to **toe**. A pulse in an **artery** was normal; so were every skin **pore**, digestive **intestine**, **stomach**, **liver**, and **kidney**. Blood tests showed no damaged **gland**, abnormal **hormone**, or weakened **immune** response. A **dental** check found only one loose tooth. Ben sat on his mother's **lap** while the doctor explained the results.

The examination also exposed careless language elsewhere in the archive. One woman with a healthy **pregnancy** had been described as carrying a “**dwarf** baby” merely because an early image looked small inside the **womb**. Another patient had been called **blind** before anyone tested her eyes. Amara explained contraception, including correct **condom** use, and warned that labels could cripple confidence even when they did not physically **cripple** a person.

Ben's hearing improved after treatment. A sound became a useful **stimulus**, and a regular **lifestyle** restored his **biorhythm** and sleep **habit**; he was soon peacefully **asleep** each night instead of producing a tired **moan**. At a hospital meeting, Amara placed the outdated file in the centre **chamber** of the archive and requested its correction. The clinic removed the insulting label and began treating patients as people rather than diagnoses.

## 中文翻译

医学生阿玛拉打开一份乡村旧病历，上面把十二岁的本写成“耳聋而且**哑的**”。本并非不会思考，也不是不能说话；一场未经治疗的**听觉系统的****感染**损害了他的听力，大人却把沉默误认为无知。一条**模糊的**记录还称他**跛脚**，然而他**脚后跟**上**明显的**伤痕表明，是鞋子过紧让他走路一瘸一拐，并不是**肌肉**残疾。

阿玛拉为他做了全面检查。本一直保持**清醒**，不过漫长课堂上会开始**打瞌睡**，放学后也常需**小睡**。她检查了从肩部到**膝盖**的每个**关节**，又测试从**大腿**到**脚趾**的每条**神经**。一条**动脉**的脉搏正常；皮肤上的每个**毛孔**、消化系统的**肠道**、**胃**、**肝脏**和**肾脏**也都正常。血液检测没有发现受损**腺体**、异常**激素**或减弱的**免疫**反应。**牙科**检查只发现一颗松动牙齿。 医生解释检查结果时，本坐在母亲的**大腿上**。

这次检查还揭示了档案中其他草率措辞。一名处于健康**妊娠期**的女性，仅因早期影像中胎儿在**子宫**里显得较小，就被写成怀着“**侏儒**婴儿”。另一位病人甚至在接受视力检查前便被称为**失明的**。阿玛拉讲解了避孕知识，包括正确使用**避孕套**，并警告说，即使标签不会使人的身体**残废**，也可能摧毁其自信。

治疗后，本的听力有所改善。声音成为有益的**刺激**；规律的**生活方式**恢复了他的**生物节律**与睡眠**习惯**，他很快每晚都能安然**入睡**，不再疲惫地发出**呻吟**。在医院会议上，阿玛拉把过时病历放在档案馆中央**房间**，要求予以更正。诊所删除了侮辱性标签，开始把病人当作人，而不是一串诊断结果。
