# 55. The Race Leo Did Not Finish（利奥没有跑完的比赛）

**主题：** 身心健康　 **目标词：** 40

Leo believed **overwork** proved strength. Despite **chronic** **insomnia**, he trained through **fatigue**, a **sore** knee, and a dark **bruise** beside an old **scar**. During a charity race, a sudden **headache** made him **faint**. His legs felt **feeble**, his vision blurred, and medics feared a **stroke** or **heart attack**. They stopped the race before exhaustion could **paralyse** him.

At hospital, Leo became an impatient **patient**. “I'm not an **invalid**,” he protested. Dr Sen agreed but explained that a seemingly **broken** body could reveal hidden trouble. Tests ruled out **cancer**, a **lethal** **infection**, and any mysterious **plague**. There was no **fever**, **flu**, dangerous **lump**, or **fatal** injury. His small **pimple** was harmless, and the swollen joint was not **arthritis**. However, he had **hypertension**, early **diabetes**, and liver **dysfunction** linked to **obesity**.

The diagnosis felt like **doom**. Leo called it **insane** that a fit-looking runner could be ill and imagined his own **demise**. Dr Sen showed him the medical **corpus** of evidence: appearance does not cancel risk. Years of stress could **exhaust** the heart, **weaken** blood vessels, and turn a manageable condition into a **mortal** danger. The emotional **trauma** of collapsing was real, but it need not determine his future.

Leo rested, changed his diet, and accepted treatment. He also learned that his skin **allergy** and occasional pain were separate issues, not signs of disaster. Months later, he returned to the race as a volunteer rather than a runner. Watching others cross the line, he no longer felt **weary** or ashamed. Not finishing one race had prevented a far more serious **detriment** to his health—and taught him that endurance includes knowing when to stop.

## 中文翻译

利奥相信**过度工作**能够证明强大。尽管长期受**慢性****失眠**困扰，他仍顶着**疲劳**、**酸痛的**膝盖以及旧**伤疤**旁的一块深色**瘀伤**坚持训练。一次慈善赛跑中，突如其来的**头痛**使他**昏倒**。他的双腿变得**虚弱**，视线模糊，医护人员担心他发生**中风**或**心脏病发作**，于是及时终止比赛，以免力竭使他**瘫痪**。

进了医院，利奥成了一个没有耐心的**病人**。“我不是**病弱者**。”他抗议道。森医生表示赞同，但解释说，看似**受损的**身体可能暴露隐藏问题。检查排除了**癌症**、**致命的****感染**以及任何神秘**瘟疫**。他没有**发烧**、**流感**、危险**肿块**或**致命的**损伤；那颗小**粉刺**没有危害，肿胀的关节也不是**关节炎**。不过，他确实患有**高血压**、早期**糖尿病**以及与**肥胖**相关的肝功能**障碍**。

这份诊断听起来如同**厄运**。利奥觉得，一个外表健壮的跑者居然会生病，简直**荒唐**，甚至想象起自己的**死亡**。森医生向他展示完整的医学证据**资料汇编**：外表不能消除风险。多年压力会**耗尽**心脏的力量，**削弱**血管，并使可控病情变成**足以致死的**危险。倒下带来的心理**创伤**确实存在，却不必决定他的未来。

利奥开始休息、改变饮食并接受治疗。他还明白，自己的皮肤**过敏**和偶尔疼痛只是独立问题，并非灾难征兆。几个月后，他以志愿者而非跑者身份重返赛场。看着其他人越过终点，他不再感到**疲倦**或羞愧。没有完成一场比赛，反而避免了健康受到更严重的**损害**，也让他懂得：真正的耐力还包括知道何时停下。
