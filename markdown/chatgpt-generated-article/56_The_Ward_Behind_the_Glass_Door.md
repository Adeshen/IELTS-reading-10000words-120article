# 56. The Ward Behind the Glass Door（玻璃门后的病房）

**主题：** 身心健康　 **目标词：** 40

**Surgeon** Elena arrived at the mountain **hospital** ready to perform a kidney **transplant**. An **X-ray** and blood test looked **agreeable**, and the donor's infection risk seemed **negligible**. Then a nurse reported a mysterious fever in the next ward. Elena's previously **merry** team grew silent. Some staff became **pessimistic**, while one administrator remained almost **indifferent**, calling the result merely **negative** publicity.

Elena treated the warning as a necessary **precaution**. She ordered the ward to **quarantine** exposed visitors, **isolate** symptomatic patients, and **segregate** clean equipment from used supplies. A **sanitary** corridor protected the operating theatre, while strict **hygiene** rules supported safe **health care**. The local **pharmacy** checked every **medicine**, from a routine **antibiotic** to **penicillin**, and prepared each **dose**. Nurses offered an **injection** and arranged to **vaccinate** vulnerable residents.

The transplant had to pause. The frightened recipient began to **lament** the delay, but Elena promised to **alleviate** his pain. She followed his **prescription**, used a careful amount of **morphine**, and offered **acupuncture** as an additional **remedy**. Physical **therapy**, gentle **fitness** work, and time to **relax** helped him remain strong. These measures were supportive rather than magically **curative**, but they allowed his body to **heal**.

Tests eventually identified a minor virus, not an outbreak. The **apathetic** administrator apologised for dismissing the danger. Elena could finally **resume** surgery, and the transplant succeeded. During the patient's steady **recovery**, his family felt pure **pleasure** and began to **rejoice**. Elena reminded them that no single treatment deserves all the credit: careful systems, skilled people, and respect for risk had opened the glass door safely.

## 中文翻译

**外科医生**埃琳娜来到山间**医院**，准备进行肾脏**移植**。一份**X光片**和血液检查结果都很**令人满意**，供体的感染风险似乎也**微不足道**。这时，一名护士报告隔壁病房出现不明发热。原本十分**快乐的**团队顿时沉默下来。有些员工变得**悲观**，一名管理人员却近乎**漠不关心**，称检查结果只会带来一点**负面的**舆论。

埃琳娜把警告视为必要的**预防措施**。她要求病区对接触者进行**隔离检疫**，将出现症状的病人单独**隔离**，并把清洁器械与使用过的物资**分开**。一条**卫生的**通道保护着手术室，严格的**卫生习惯**规则则保障安全的**医疗保健**。当地**药房**核查每一种**药物**，从常规**抗生素**到**青霉素**，并准备好每一份**剂量**。护士提供了**注射**治疗，并安排为易感居民**接种疫苗**。

移植手术不得不暂停。惊恐的受术者开始**哀叹**延期，埃琳娜则承诺会**减轻**他的疼痛。她遵照**处方**，谨慎使用一定量的**吗啡**，并把**针灸**作为辅助**疗法**。身体**治疗**、温和的**健身**活动以及适当**放松**，帮助他保持体力。这些措施是支持性的，并非具有神奇**治愈作用的**手段，却使他的身体逐渐**愈合**。

检查最终确认那只是一种轻微病毒，而非疫情暴发。那名曾经**冷漠的**管理人员为轻视危险而道歉。埃琳娜终于能够**重新开始**手术，移植获得成功。病人平稳**康复**期间，家人感受到纯粹的**喜悦**，开始共同**欢庆**。埃琳娜提醒他们，功劳不属于某一种治疗：严密的制度、专业的人以及对风险的尊重，共同安全地打开了那扇玻璃门。
