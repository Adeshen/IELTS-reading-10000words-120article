# 57. The Doctor Who Climbed for a Library（为一座图书馆登山的医生）

**主题：** 身心健康　 **目标词：** 40

Dr Yusuf's mountain village was **picturesque** and **hospitable**, but its clinic owned only three books. He proposed an **arduous** sponsored climb to fund a library. The idea was **surprising** enough to **startle** his colleagues: Yusuf was a **prominent** surgeon, yet not a **robust** athlete. Still, his **earnest** manner, quiet **confidence**, and **keen** desire to learn attracted a **lively** team. His obvious **honesty** soon won the villagers' trust.

Training became **hectic** but **exciting**. A **liberal** headteacher welcomed every volunteer; a **humble** farmer offered food; and a **polite**, **friendly** guide taught climbing skills. He was **frank** about the danger and **direct** in every instruction. One **curious** student asked about a **mysterious** light near the summit, while a **daring** reporter called the expedition “**manly**.” Yusuf replied that courage had no gender and should include being **thoughtful** and **sympathetic** toward weaker climbers.

During the ascent, falling ice produced an **amazing** roar that seemed to **astound** the valley. The sight could **exhilarate** anyone, but Yusuf took the risk **seriously**. When a climber slipped, the whole team was **ready**. Their **sound** rope system held, and Yusuf's calm care restored morale. His **zeal** never replaced caution.

The group reached the summit at sunrise. The achievement was **remarkable**, yet Yusuf was most **proud** when villagers opened boxes of books below. Donations exceeded the **satisfactory** target, making a children's science room both **desirable** and possible. A young teacher thanked him with sincere **gratitude**; a donor jokingly offered a **romantic** novel for the first shelf. The library's future looked **promising**, not because one doctor conquered a mountain, but because an entire community climbed with him.

## 中文翻译

优素福医生的山村景色**如画**，村民也十分**好客**，可诊所只有三本书。他提出举行一次**艰苦的**赞助登山活动，为图书馆筹款。这个想法十分**出人意料**，把同事们都**吓了一跳**：优素福是位**杰出的**外科医生，却不是一名**强健的**运动员。不过，他**诚恳的**态度、安静的**自信**和**热切的**求知愿望，还是吸引了一支**充满活力的**队伍。 他显而易见的**诚实**很快赢得了村民的信任。

训练生活变得**忙碌**却又**令人兴奋**。一位思想**开明的**校长欢迎所有志愿者；一位**谦逊的**农民提供食物；一名**礼貌而****友好的**向导则教授登山技能。他对危险十分**坦率**，每一项指令也都很**直接**。一名**好奇的**学生询问山顶附近的**神秘**亮光，一名**大胆的**记者则称这次远征“很有**男子气概**”。优素福回答，勇气不分性别，还应当包括对体力较弱者保持**体贴**与**同情**。

登山途中，坠冰发出**惊人的**轰鸣，仿佛要让整座山谷都为之**震惊**。眼前景象足以使人**振奋**，优素福却**认真地**对待风险。一名队员滑倒时，全队早已**准备好**应对。**可靠的**绳索系统牢牢托住了他，优素福冷静的照顾也恢复了士气。他的**热情**始终没有取代谨慎。

队伍在日出时抵达山顶。这项成就十分**非凡**，但当山下村民打开一箱箱书时，优素福才感到最为**自豪**。捐款超过了**令人满意的**目标，使儿童科学室成为既**理想**又可实现的计划。一名年轻教师带着真挚的**感激**向他道谢；一位捐赠者还开玩笑地送来一本**浪漫的**小说，放上第一层书架。图书馆的未来显得充满**希望**，并不是因为某一位医生征服了山峰，而是因为整个社区与他一同向上攀登。
