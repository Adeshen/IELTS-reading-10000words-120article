# 59. The Rehearsal No One Wanted（无人愿意参加的排练）

**主题：** 身心健康　 **目标词：** 40

Director Bram ruled the community theatre through fear. He would **harass** junior actors, **insult** anyone who forgot a line, and **vex** the crew with **excessive** rehearsals. His jokes were **vulgar**, his criticism **nasty**, and his temper **volatile**. Under **adverse** conditions, even a small delay made him **mad**. Some called his methods **wicked** and **vicious**; others stayed silent because his **ego** made him **hostile** to advice. Everyone found the criticism deeply **unpleasant**.

During rehearsal, Bram accused timid actor Noah of being **slothful**, **careless**, and **stupid**. Noah was not lazy: a **weird** lighting flash had left him **uneasy** and **restless**. Bram remained **oblivious** to the faulty cable and made an **unkind** remark about Noah's **timid** voice. Costume designer Aya became **indignant**. She said Bram's behaviour was an **obtrusive** **nuisance**, not artistic discipline, and demanded a **radical** change.

Bram dismissed her concern as a **fuss**. His **rash**, **selfish** decision to continue made the unstable lamp fall. No one was hurt, but **suspicion** turned to proof: the equipment was **unstable** and the room **unsuitable** for rehearsal. Even Bram's most **reluctant** supporters refused to **endure** another unsafe night. They were not **ignorant**; nor would his **cunning** excuses mislead them.

The board suspended Bram. At first he felt **jealous** of Aya's leadership and called the decision **unsatisfactory**. Weeks later, he returned to **repent** publicly. Aya did not offer instant forgiveness, but she did offer **tolerance** if he accepted supervision. Bram agreed. The next production succeeded because every performer could question a decision without fear. The theatre lost one tyrant, and Bram slowly learned that respect creates better art than intimidation.

## 中文翻译

导演布拉姆依靠恐惧统治社区剧院。他会**骚扰**年轻演员，**侮辱**任何忘词的人，还用**过度的**排练**惹恼**剧组。他的玩笑十分**粗俗**，批评也很**恶毒**，脾气更是**反复无常**。在**不利的**条件下，一点延误都能使他**发疯般愤怒**。有人称他的方法**邪恶**而**凶残**；另一些人则保持沉默，因为他的**自我意识**使他对建议抱有**敌意**。 所有人都觉得这种批评十分**令人不快**。

排练期间，布拉姆指责腼腆的演员诺亚**懒惰**、**粗心**而且**愚蠢**。诺亚并非偷懒：一次**怪异的**灯光闪烁使他**不安**、**坐立不安**。布拉姆仍**没有察觉**故障电缆，还针对诺亚**胆怯的**声音说了一句**刻薄的**话。服装设计师阿雅十分**愤慨**。她说，布拉姆的行为是一种**显眼而烦人的****麻烦**，不是艺术纪律，并要求进行**彻底的**改变。

布拉姆把她的担忧斥为**小题大做**。他**轻率而****自私的**决定使排练继续，结果不稳的灯具坠落下来。虽然无人受伤，但原先的**怀疑**变成了证据：设备确实**不稳定**，房间也**不适合**排练。就连最**不情愿的**支持者也拒绝继续**忍受**不安全的夜晚。他们并不**无知**，更不会再被布拉姆**狡猾的**借口误导。

董事会暂停了布拉姆的职务。起初，他**嫉妒**阿雅的领导地位，称决定**不能令人满意**。几周后，他回来公开**悔过**。阿雅没有立刻原谅他，但如果他愿意接受监督，她可以给予**宽容**。布拉姆同意了。下一部作品获得成功，因为每一位表演者都能毫无畏惧地质疑决定。剧院摆脱了一名暴君，而布拉姆也逐渐明白，尊重比恐吓更能创造好作品。
