# 60. The Clock That Would Not Hurry（不肯快走的钟）

**主题：** 身心健康 / 时间日期　 **目标词：** 40

In the **modern** **era**, clockmaker Mara maintained a **medieval** tower clock that had marked **midday** for nearly a **century**. A **recent** inspection found its mechanism **sluggish**, and the mayor demanded an **up-to-date** motor **overnight**. He followed a **rigid** **stereotype**: old machines were slow, while new ones solved everything in an **instant**. Mara resisted the **impulse** to argue.

She reviewed the **preceding** repair and all **prior** records. **Initially**, twelve gears—a **dozen**—seemed worn. **Secondly**, the bell had missed three **consecutive** strikes. Most importantly, a cracked **intermediate** wheel could fail at any **moment**. There was no exact **precedent** for replacing it, because the **foremost** surviving design dated from another **millennium**. A note claimed the clock had ticked a **billion** times, though Mara knew that figure was symbolic.

In the **midst** of repairs, an **imminent** festival increased pressure. The clock's **daily**, **monthly**, **quarterly**, and **yearly** maintenance logs all needed review. In the **meantime**, the mayor scheduled **simultaneous** fireworks and a bell ceremony. Mara warned that the **ongoing** restoration required a realistic **duration**. She could not promise an **everlasting** machine, only careful work and **periodically** inspected parts.

Mara completed the gear just before noon, but refused to **finish** until she tested it. At the decisive **moment**, the hands met and the bell rang precisely. The mayor, normally **punctual**, arrived late and missed the first chime. **Incidentally**, his expensive motor remained unopened. The successful repair showed that age was not the enemy of accuracy; neglect was. The clock would never hurry, but with patience it would continue to keep honest time.

## 中文翻译

在这个**现代的****时代**，钟表匠玛拉负责维护一座**中世纪的**塔钟；近一个**世纪**以来，它一直在**正午**报时。一次**最近的**检查发现机械装置运转**迟缓**，市长却要求**一夜之间**装上一台**最新的**电动机。他抱着一种**僵化的****刻板印象**：旧机器一定缓慢，新机器则能在**瞬间**解决一切。玛拉忍住了争辩的**冲动**。

她查阅了**上一次**维修情况和所有**先前的**记录。**起初**，有十二个齿轮——整整**一打**——看似已经磨损。**其次**，钟铃连续漏敲了三次，也就是三次**连续的**失误。最重要的是，一个开裂的**中间**齿轮随时可能在某个**时刻**失效。更换它没有完全相同的**先例**，因为现存**最重要的**设计来自另一个**千年时期**。一份说明声称塔钟已摆动**十亿**次，不过玛拉知道这个数字只是象征。

维修进行到**中途**时，**即将到来的**节庆加大了压力。塔钟的**每日**、**每月**、**每季度**和**每年**维护记录都需要复查。**在此期间**，市长安排烟花与钟声仪式**同时**举行。玛拉提醒他，这项**持续进行的**修复需要合理的**持续时间**。她无法承诺一台**永恒不坏的**机器，只能保证认真工作，并**定期地**检查零件。

玛拉在中午前完成了齿轮，却拒绝在测试前宣布**完成**。决定性的**一刻**，指针相遇，钟声准确响起。平日一向**准时的**市长反而迟到，错过了第一声钟响。**顺便一提**，他买来的昂贵电动机仍未拆封。这次成功维修证明，年龄并不是准确性的敌人，疏忽才是。塔钟永远不会匆忙，但只要有耐心，它就会继续诚实地记录时间。
